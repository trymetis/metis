---
title: Move and resize events
description: Drag an event to a new time or day, or drag its bottom edge to make it longer or shorter.
---

In Day and Week view you can change an event's time with the mouse. Metis Suite saves the change to your calendar provider as soon as you let go.

## Move an event

1. Open the calendar in **Day** or **Week** view.
2. Drag the event up or down to a new time.
3. In Week view, you can also drag it sideways onto another day.
4. Let go. The event keeps its length.

## Change how long an event lasts

1. Open the calendar in **Day** or **Week** view.
2. Point at the bottom edge of the event.
3. Drag the edge down to make the event longer, or up to make it shorter.
4. Let go.

## Things to know

- An event moves, or grows and shrinks, in steps of 15 minutes.
- An event cannot be shorter than 15 minutes.
- You can drag only events that have a start and end time. Events in the **All day** row cannot be dragged.
- In Month view you cannot drag events. To change an event there, open it and click **Edit event**. See [View, edit and delete events](/guide/calendar/view-edit-and-delete-events).
- After you let go, Metis Suite saves the change and the calendar refreshes. If the change could not be saved, an error message appears and the event stays in its old place.
