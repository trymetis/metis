---
title: Workflows
description: Automate work across your mail, calendars, drives and contacts with workflows you build on a canvas.
---

A workflow is a set of steps that Metis Suite follows for you. Something starts it, such as new mail, a schedule, a calendar event, a new file or a request from another service. Then each step does one job: it reads, checks, changes, sends, waits, asks a person or asks AI.

For example, a workflow can save the PDF from every invoice email to a drive, or mail you a summary of today's meetings every weekday at 8:00.

Workflows run in the background on your server, so they keep working when Metis Suite is closed.

## Build workflows

- [Use the editor](/guide/workflows/use-the-editor): add, connect, copy and remove steps, and fix the problems the editor finds.
- [Triggers](/guide/workflows/triggers): every way a workflow can start, including schedules, mail, calendar and drive changes, and app events.
- [Steps](/guide/workflows/steps): every step you can add, what it does and the settings it has.
- [Expressions](/guide/workflows/expressions): use values from earlier steps, write conditions and fill in text.
- [Let AI decide with Ask and Branch](/guide/workflows/ask-and-branch): ask an AI model questions about each item and route it by the answer.
- [Ask for approval](/guide/workflows/approvals): make a workflow wait until a person approves or denies, and answer requests.
- [Start a workflow from a webhook](/guide/workflows/webhooks): let another service start a workflow by sending it a request.
- [Add a BCC to outgoing mail](/guide/workflows/add-a-bcc-to-outgoing-mail): copy the mail you send to another address.

- [Run a workflow and check its history](/guide/workflows/run-and-check-history): run a workflow now, then see what each run did.
- [Cancel and retry runs](/guide/workflows/cancel-and-retry-runs): stop a run, retry the steps that failed, and resolve steps whose outcome is unconfirmed.
- [Manage workflows](/guide/workflows/manage-workflows): edit workflows, change their settings, turn them on and off, delete them, and change them from the assistant.
- [Limits](/guide/workflows/limits): the sizes, rules and problem messages every workflow has to fit.
