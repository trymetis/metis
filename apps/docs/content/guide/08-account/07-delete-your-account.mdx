---
title: Delete your account
description: Permanently delete your Metis Suite account and what Metis Suite stores for you.
---

Deleting your account removes you from Metis Suite for good. Read this page to the end before you start.

:::warning[This cannot be undone]
Once your account is deleted, you cannot get it back.
:::

## What gets deleted

Metis Suite permanently deletes your account and almost everything it stores for you (see [What stays for a while](#what-stays-for-a-while)):

- the workspaces that only you belong to
- the mailboxes, calendars and drives you connected
- your labels
- your workflows
- your scheduled sends

Your mail itself stays with your email providers, such as Gmail or Outlook. Metis Suite does not delete anything from your accounts there.

Where the provider supports it, Metis Suite also takes back the access you gave it to your connected accounts.

### What stays for a while

Some changes are recorded for security, such as removing a member or turning off two-factor authentication. See [Security activity](/guide/account/security-activity). The record of the changes you made stays after your account is gone. It is deleted when the time set by the person who runs your Metis Suite server runs out. Until then:

- The server owner's audit log keeps every recorded change you made, with your email address. This includes the deletion of your account.
- Workspace owners and admins still see the changes you made in their workspace, on its **Activity** page.
- People whose role you changed, whom you removed, or whose mailbox, calendar or drive you detached still see that change in their own **Security activity**.

## Before you start

- **Download a copy of your data.** On the **Account** settings page, click **Download export**. The file holds your preferences, workspaces, labels, workflows, Workspace Assistant chats and security activity. It does not hold your mail or your scheduled sends. See [Export your data](/guide/account/export-your-data).
- **If you connected Outlook**, also remove Metis Suite from your Microsoft account. The **Delete account** card has links for a personal Microsoft account and for a work or school account.
- **If you own a workspace that other people use**, make someone else its owner first. Click the workspace tile at the top of the left rail, choose **General**, then click **Manage members**. Click the new owner, click **Transfer ownership** and enter your code. See [Transfer ownership](/guide/workspaces/members-and-roles#transfer-ownership). If you skip this, you see "You're the owner of" followed by the number of workspaces, and you are asked to transfer ownership first.
- **Have your authenticator app ready**, or a backup code. The last step asks for a code.

## Delete your account

1. Open your user menu (the round button with your initials at the bottom of the left rail) and click **Settings**.
2. Click **Security**, then scroll down to the red **Delete account** card.

     alt="The red Delete account card at the bottom of the Security settings page"
     class="dark:hidden"
   />
   <img
     src="/images/account/delete-account-dark.webp"
     alt="The red Delete account card at the bottom of the Security settings page"
     class="hidden dark:block"

3. Type your email address exactly as shown in the field, to confirm.
4. Click **Delete account**.
5. The box **Delete your account?** asks you to confirm. Click **Continue**.
6. The box **Enter your authenticator code** opens. Enter the code from your authenticator app, or a backup code.
7. Click **Delete account**.

Metis Suite deletes your account and signs you out.

If you see "Too many attempts. Please slow down.", wait a little and try again.
