---
title: Contacts
description: Browse, create, edit and delete the contacts in the address books connected to your workspace, and save people from your mail.
---

The Contacts section shows the people in the address books your workspace can use. An address book is the contact list of one connected account, such as your Google Contacts, your Outlook Contacts, your Zoho Contacts or a contacts server your administrator set up. Your contacts stay in that account. Metis Suite shows them and makes your changes there, so the change also shows in every other app that uses the same address book.

- [Browse your contacts](/guide/contacts/browse-your-contacts): open Contacts, switch address books, filter the list and open a contact's card.
- [Create, edit and delete contacts](/guide/contacts/create-edit-and-delete): add a new contact, change one, or delete it.
- [Save contacts from mail](/guide/contacts/save-contacts-from-mail): save the people who write to you, and where your saved contacts show up.
- [Show and hide address books](/guide/contacts/show-and-hide-address-books): choose which of an account's address books a workspace shows.
- [Add an address book](/guide/contacts/add-an-address-book): connect a contacts account and add it to a workspace.
- [Contacts AI](/guide/contacts/contacts-ai): ask the assistant about your contacts.
- [Address book types](/guide/contacts/address-book-types): what differs between Google, Outlook, Zoho and contacts servers.

To add a contacts account to a workspace, you can also start from [Workspaces](/guide/workspaces).
