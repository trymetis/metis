title: AI assistant
description: Ask the assistant to help with your mail, calendar, files and contacts, approve what it does, and connect outside AI apps with MCP.
---

Metis Suite has AI assistants that read, search and act on your mail, calendars, drives and contacts. Workspace AI works across your whole workspace, and the Mail, Calendar, Drive and Contacts AI panels help with the page you are on. You can also connect an outside AI app through MCP. Start with how AI works, then pick the page for what you want to do.

- [How AI works in Metis Suite](/guide/assistant/how-ai-works): the five assistants, the switches that control them, and what stays private.
- [Use Workspace AI](/guide/assistant/use-workspace-ai): ask across your workspace, point at exact items with @, and find past conversations.
- [Use the Mail, Calendar, Drive and Contacts AI panels](/guide/assistant/use-the-side-panels): quick help beside a mailbox, calendar, drive or address book, with attached emails, events, files and contacts.
- [Approve or decline actions](/guide/assistant/approve-or-decline-actions): how the assistant asks first, and which actions need your approval.
- [What the assistant can do](/guide/assistant/what-the-assistant-can-do): everything each assistant can read and change, and what it never does.
- [Limits and troubleshooting](/guide/assistant/limits-and-troubleshooting): what the on-screen messages mean and how to fix common problems.
- [Connect an AI client with MCP](/guide/assistant/connect-an-ai-client): add Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Codex, Cursor, VS Code or another app, step by step.
- [Manage MCP clients](/guide/assistant/manage-mcp-clients): choose a client's tools and workspaces, revoke sign-ins, turn it off or remove it.
- [What an MCP client can do](/guide/assistant/what-an-mcp-client-can-do): the tools a client can use, which are sensitive, and the rules it follows.
