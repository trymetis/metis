---
title: Manage MCP clients
description: Choose a client's tools and workspaces, see its sign-ins, turn it off, or remove it.
---

Every AI app you connect through MCP has its own entry under **Settings**, **MCP clients**. Each client belongs to you alone, not to a workspace. A client that is not on your list has no access to your account.

## The client list

Each row in the list shows:

- The client's icon and name, with a **Disabled** badge if you turned it off. A client you added as **Other client**, and an app that identified itself, show the Model Context Protocol logo.
- For a known app, which app it is (for example **Claude Code**). Then, for every client, when you added it (for example "added 2 days ago"), when it was last used (or "never used"), how many of your workspaces it can reach, and, once you have approved it, "authorized" or how many authorizations it holds.
- A switch to turn the client on or off.
- A trash button (**Remove client**).

Click a row to open the client's own page.

## A client's page

The top of the page shows the client's name, its details, and its **Client ID** with a **Copy client ID** button. Below that are three sections.

### Tools

Choose which tools this client may use. Unchecking a tool blocks this client from using it at all. Your other clients are not affected.

The tools are split into two groups:

- **Non-sensitive**: read and organize your mail, calendars, drives and contacts. These are on for a new client.
- **Sensitive**: send, delete, change events, files and contacts, run workflows and manage members. These are off for a new client.

Each group has a checkbox that turns every tool in it on or off, and a count of how many are on. When you expand a group, it shows a search box: **Search non-sensitive tools** or **Search sensitive tools**. The search matches a tool's name, its description or the heading it is listed under. If nothing matches, you see "No non-sensitive tool matches “send”" or "No sensitive tool matches “send”" (with what you typed). Click the X in the box (**Clear search**) to empty it. Click **Save** when you are done. For the full list, see [What an MCP client can do](/guide/assistant/what-an-mcp-client-can-do).

:::warning
MCP clients never ask for approval in a chat. When you turn on a sensitive tool, such as sending email, the client can use it whenever it wants. Turn on only what you need.
:::

### Workspaces

Choose which of your workspaces this client can reach. Each workspace has a switch, labelled with your role there. Changes save straight away.

- A workspace you join later is reachable too, until you turn it off here.
- A workspace with AI turned off shows "Unavailable" and its switch cannot be used. No client can reach it.
- Hiding a workspace is a convenience, not a security measure. To cut a client off completely, remove it.

### Authorizations

Each time you approve the client's sign-in, it gets an authorization. This section lists each one, with when you approved it and when it expires. If the client has none, the section shows "Not authorized. The client will ask for consent the next time it connects."

- **Revoke** ends one authorization. The client must sign in again on its next request. If it has other authorizations, it signs back in through them without asking you. If that was its last one, it must ask for your approval again, and you confirm it's you when you approve it.
- **Revoke all** (shown when there is more than one) ends every authorization. A box titled, for example, **Revoke every authorization for Claude Code?** says "All 2 authorizations stop working immediately, and the client must ask for your approval again, which you confirm with your authenticator app, a passkey or a backup code. The client itself stays connected, so you won’t need to reconfigure it." Click **Revoke all** in the box. The client stays on your list, so you do not need to set it up again in your app.

After a revoke, the client's next request is refused with "This access token was revoked. Sign in again." Your AI app may show this message or ask you to sign in again.

Changes to tools and workspaces apply from the client's next request.

## Turn a client off or back on

Use the switch next to the client, in the list or on its page.

- **Off:** the client stops working at once. Its requests are refused with "This MCP client is disabled or no longer registered."
- **On again:** Metis Suite asks you to confirm it's you. A dialog opens, titled "Re-enable" followed by the client's name, for example **Re-enable Claude Code**. It says "Re-enabling lets this client sign back in on its own, even after you log out, so confirm it’s you first." Type a code from your authenticator app. The field opens on **Use your passkey** instead when **Ask me first with** is set to **Passkey**, or when you have passkeys and no authenticator app, in a browser that supports passkeys. Links under the field switch to the other ways your account has: **Use your authenticator instead**, **Use a passkey instead** and **Use a backup code instead**. Then click **Enable**. The button says "Verifying…" while it checks. To leave the client off, click **Cancel**.

If the instance owner or an instance admin bans your account, all your clients stop working too. While the ban lasts, their requests are refused with "This account is banned. Ask the instance owner or an admin." They work again once the ban ends. A client may ask you to sign in again first.

## Remove a client

1. Click the trash button (**Remove client**) in the list or on the client's page.
2. Read the dialog. It shows the client ID and, under **Also remove it locally**, how to remove Metis Suite from your app:
   - For Claude Code and Codex CLI, "Run this in your terminal" shows a command with a copy button: `claude mcp remove metis-suite` or `codex mcp remove metis-suite`.
   - For Claude Desktop or claude.ai, "Do this in the client" says "In Settings → Connectors, remove the Metis Suite connector."
   - For Cursor and VS Code, "Do this in the client" says, for example, "Open Cursor's mcp.json and delete the "metis-suite" entry."
   - For other clients, it says "Remove the Metis Suite server from your client's MCP settings."

   Steps you do in the client have no copy button.

3. Click **Remove**.

The client's access ends at once and its sign-ins are deleted. To connect it again, you have to add it again. Removing it here does not change your AI app's own settings, so also remove Metis Suite from the app. Otherwise the app keeps trying to connect and fails.

## Changes that are recorded

Adding a client, approving a client, turning a client back on, and turning on sensitive tools are recorded in your [Security activity](/guide/account/security-activity). Each row names the client and lists any sensitive tools that were turned on.

## Common messages

| Message                                                               | What it means                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Invalid authenticator or backup code"                                | The authenticator or backup code was wrong or expired. Type a new one.                                                                                                                                  |
| "Your passkey confirmation expired. Confirm with your passkey again." | Too much time passed after you confirmed with your passkey. Click **Confirm again** under **Passkey confirmed**, use your passkey, then click the button again.                                         |
| "Confirm it's you to re-enable this client"                           | Confirm with your authenticator app, a passkey or a backup code before you click **Enable**.                                                                                                            |
| "Too many attempts. Try again in 4 minutes."                          | You tried to confirm too many times. Adding a client, approving a client and turning a client back on each allow 10 tries every 5 minutes. Wait for the time shown (at most 5 minutes), then try again. |
| "Claude Code needs a fixed sign-in port."                             | Fill in **Sign-in port** when you add a Claude Code client.                                                                                                                                             |
| "At least one callback URL is required"                               | Fill in **Sign-in return URL** when you add an **Other client**.                                                                                                                                        |
| "Could not add the client"                                            | Something went wrong. Try again.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| "This access token was revoked. Sign in again."                       | Your AI app may show this after you revoke its authorizations. Approve its sign-in again, or leave it signed out.                                                                                       |

If your AI app reports that it is sending too many requests, wait for the time it gives (at most 1 minute) and try again. Each client can make up to 120 requests a minute. See [What an MCP client can do](/guide/assistant/what-an-mcp-client-can-do#rules-every-client-follows).
