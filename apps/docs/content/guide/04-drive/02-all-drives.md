---
title: All drives
description: See every drive in the workspace in one view, and upload or create folders from there.
---

**All drives** puts every drive in the workspace into one view. It is useful when your workspace has several drives and you do not remember which one holds a file.

## Open All drives

Click the drive switcher at the top of the sidebar and choose **All drives**. The option shows only when the workspace has more than one drive. While you are in All drives, the switcher shows **All drives** and the number of drives.

## What you see

- **At the top level**, each drive appears as one folder, named with the email address of its account.
- **Inside one of those folders**, you work in that drive just as you would on its own page.
- **The breadcrumbs** start with **All drives**. Click it to return to the top level.
- **The search box** searches every drive at once. See [Search](/guide/drive/search).
- **The storage line** in the sidebar says "Storage unavailable". Open a single drive to see its storage use.

## Upload or create a folder from the top level

At the top level there is no folder to put things in yet, so Metis asks you where they should go.

1. Click **Upload** and choose your files, or click **New folder**.
2. A dialog opens and asks where to put them:
   - For an upload, the dialog is titled **Upload**. It names your file (or the number of files) and asks which folder to upload into.
   - For a new folder, the dialog is titled **New folder**. It says "Choose the folder to create it in."
3. The dialog starts at **Drives**, which lists each drive by its type or server name and its email address. Click a drive to open it, then click folders to go deeper. Use the path at the top of the dialog to go back.
4. Click **Upload here** or **Create here**. The button stays off until you have opened a drive.
5. For a new folder, type its name in the next dialog and click **Create**.

<img src="/images/drive/all-drives-picker.webp" alt="The New folder dialog asking which folder to create it in, starting at the list of drives" class="dark:hidden" />
<img src="/images/drive/all-drives-picker-dark.webp" alt="The New folder dialog asking which folder to create it in, starting at the list of drives" class="hidden dark:block" />

If the dialog cannot read your drives, it says "Could not read your drives". A drive or folder with nothing in it shows "Nothing here".

Inside a drive's folder, **Upload** and **New folder** work directly on the folder you are in, as they do on the drive's own page.
