---
title: Sign in for the first time
description: Create your Metis Suite account, set up two-factor authentication and open your first workspace.
---

In this tutorial you will create your Metis Suite account, protect it with two-factor authentication, and open your first workspace.

You need two things before you start:

- **A way in.** This is an invite email from someone who already uses Metis Suite. If you are the owner of a new instance, it is the setup link and setup code from whoever installed it.
- **An authenticator app or a passkey.** An authenticator app runs on your phone, such as Google Authenticator, 1Password or Authy. A passkey is a sign-in key that your device keeps for you. You can make one with Touch ID, Windows Hello, a phone, a security key or a password manager. Two-factor authentication is required for every account, so you cannot skip this part.

:::note[What is an instance?]
An instance is one Metis Suite server, run by your company, your team or yourself. Everyone on an instance signs in at the same web address. Your administrator is the person who runs it.
:::

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    How you create your account depends on how you got here. Pick the tab that matches.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="I was invited">
        Open the invite email and click its button. An invite to create an account has a **Create your account** button. An invite to a workspace has an **Accept invite** button.

        A page opens with a heading such as "Create your account" or "Join Design as a member", and a line that says who invited you. Your **Email address** is already filled in and cannot be changed. Enter your **Name** and a **Password** (at least 8 characters). If your instance has terms, tick the box to agree to them. Then click **Create account**.

        You do not need to verify your email. The invite already proves the address is yours.

        If you see **Invite unavailable** with "This invite link is no longer valid", the invite has expired, was already used or was revoked. Invites last 7 days. Ask the person who invited you to send a new one. For the other messages, see [How invites work](/guide/account/invite-people#if-the-invite-does-not-work).
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="I have a setup code">
        Open the setup link your administrator gave you. The page **Set up Metis Suite** says "Create the owner account for this instance."

        Fill in the form:

        - **Setup code**: paste the setup code your administrator gave you.
        - **Name**: your name.
        - **Email address**: the owner's address, already filled in by your administrator. You cannot change it.
        - **Password**: at least 8 characters.

        If your instance has terms, tick the box to agree to them. Then click **Create account**.

        The setup page works only while no account has the owner's address. If you see "That setup code isn't valid", check that you copied the whole code. If the setup link takes you to the sign-in page, the owner account already exists. Sign in instead.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    Either way, Metis Suite creates your account and a personal workspace named after you, such as "Ana's workspace". If you were invited to a workspace, you also join it. Then Metis Suite takes you straight to two-factor setup.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm your password">
    The page **Set up two-factor authentication** opens. It says "Required to protect your Metis Suite account."

    Two-factor authentication means that signing in needs two things. First you type your password. Then you confirm it's you with a code from an authenticator app or with a passkey. Someone who steals your password still cannot get in without it.

    The page says "Confirm your password, then set up an authenticator app or a passkey." Type the password you just chose. Then pick how you want to confirm sign-ins:

    - **Continue** sets up an authenticator app.
    - **Set up a passkey instead** sets up a passkey. This button shows only if your browser supports passkeys.

    If you see "Enter your account password", type the password you chose (at least 8 characters) and try again.

    You can add the other kind later. Until you finish this setup, every page in Metis Suite brings you back here.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up an authenticator app or a passkey">
    Pick the tab that matches the button you clicked.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Authenticator app">
        1. A QR code appears. Open your authenticator app on your phone and add a new account. Point the camera at the QR code on the screen. If you cannot scan it, click **Can't scan? Show manual key** and type the key into your app instead.
        2. Below the QR code is a card called **Save these backup codes**. Backup codes let you sign in if you lose your phone. Each one works only once. Click **Download .txt** to save them as a file, or **Copy** to paste them somewhere safe, such as a password manager. Do not keep them only on the same phone as your authenticator app.
        3. When they are saved, click **I've saved my backup codes, continue**.
        4. The page says "Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app to finish setup." Type the code your app shows right now. The code keeps changing, so always use the current one.
        5. Click **Verify and enable**.

        If you see "Invalid code", wait for your app to show a new code and try again. You can click **Back** to see the QR code again.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Passkey">
        1. The page says "Keep these codes somewhere safe before you create the passkey. They get you in if you lose access to it." Your browser does not ask for a passkey yet.
        2. Below it is a card called **Save these backup codes**. It says "Each can be used once if you can't use your authenticator app or passkey." Click **Download .txt** to save them as a file, or **Copy** to paste them somewhere safe, such as a password manager.
        3. When they are saved, click **I've saved my backup codes, continue**.
        4. Your browser or device asks you to create the passkey. While it waits, the button says "Waiting for your passkey…". Create the passkey the way it asks, for example with your fingerprint, your face, a PIN or your security key. It can be kept by your device, your phone, a security key or a password manager.

        Two-factor authentication turns on only once the passkey is saved.

        Metis Suite names the passkey after the password manager that keeps it, when it recognizes it. Otherwise the name is **Passkey**. You can rename it later. See [Rename a passkey](/guide/account/two-factor-authentication#rename-a-passkey).

        If something goes wrong, a message shows above the button. There is no way back to the password step from here:

        - "The passkey request was cancelled or timed out." or "The passkey request was cancelled.": you closed the prompt, or waited too long. Click **I've saved my backup codes, continue** again. Your backup codes stay the same.
        - "Couldn't verify the new passkey", "The passkey request expired. Try again.", "Couldn't save the passkey" or "Couldn't set up the passkey": click **I've saved my backup codes, continue** again. You get a fresh set of backup codes, with the note "Here is a fresh set of backup codes. Save these instead of the earlier ones, then continue." The earlier codes stop working. Save the new ones, then click the button again to create the passkey.

        If you wait a few minutes before you click the button, you also get a fresh set of backup codes with the same note. Save them, then click the button again.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    Either way, you see **Two-factor enabled** and "Taking you to your inbox…". Metis Suite then takes you into your workspace.

    :::warning[If you lose your device]
    Your backup codes are your way back in if you lose the phone with your authenticator app, or the device with your passkey. Store them somewhere other than that device. If you lose your authenticator app or passkey and your backup codes, you cannot get back into your account, and there is no recovery inside Metis Suite. Keep your backup codes safe.
    :::

  </Step>

  <Step title="Look around your first workspace">
    A workspace is where your mailboxes, calendars, drives and address books live. Your default workspace is the one signing in always takes you to. It is your personal workspace, or the workspace you were invited to if you came from a workspace invite.

    Your personal workspace starts empty, so there you see the page **Nothing in this workspace yet**. It offers an **Add mailbox** button. You can also add a calendar, drive or address book from the settings menu. If you came from a workspace invite, you start in that workspace instead, and it may already hold mailboxes, calendars, drives or address books that its members added.

    From now on, signing in opens the inbox of your first mailbox. If the workspace has no mailbox, it opens your calendar. If there is no calendar either, it opens your drive, and then your contacts.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Next time you sign in

Go to your instance's web address, enter your email and password, and click **Sign in**. Then enter the code from your authenticator app, or use your passkey. [Sign in](/guide/account/sign-in) covers this, plus magic links and backup codes.

Once you are in, you can add a passkey or an authenticator app, whichever you did not set up, under **Settings**, then **Security**. See [Add a passkey](/guide/account/two-factor-authentication#add-a-passkey) and [Set up an authenticator app](/guide/account/two-factor-authentication#set-up-an-authenticator-app).
