New to Metis Suite? Follow these pages in order. By the end, you will have an account, know where everything is, and see your own mail in Metis Suite.

1. [Sign in for the first time](/guide/get-started/sign-in-for-the-first-time): create your account, set up two-factor authentication and open your first workspace.
2. [Find your way around](/guide/get-started/find-your-way-around): the left rail, workspaces, the account menu and settings.
3. [Connect your first mailbox](/guide/get-started/connect-your-first-mailbox): bring your email into Metis Suite.

Then, to find anything in your workspace from one box, see [Search](/guide/search).
