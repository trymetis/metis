This page covers everything about writing mail: a new message, replies and forwards, attachments, and what happens after you click **Send**. For signatures and the workspace footer, see [Signatures and the mail footer](/guide/mail/signatures).

## Write a new message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the composer">
    Click **Compose** in the sidebar, or press **m** then **c**. The composer opens as a window over
    your mail. Its title is **New message**, and the cursor starts in **To**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the From address">
    If your workspace has more than one mailbox, **From** is a menu. It starts on the mailbox you
    are in (or the first mailbox in **All inboxes**). With one mailbox, **From** just shows its
    address. You can change **From** only until the draft is first saved (see [Drafts](#drafts)), so
    pick it before you start typing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add recipients">
    Type an address in **To**. Metis Suite suggests people as you type. Your saved
    [contacts](/guide/contacts) come first, then people from your recent mail. Someone you just
    emailed usually shows up right away. Other new people from your mail can take up to 10 minutes.
    An address becomes a chip when you type a comma or semicolon, press Enter, or click away. Click
    **Cc** or **Bcc** to add those fields too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a subject and write your message">
    Type the subject in **Subject line…** and your message in **Write your message…**. If there is a
    default signature for new messages, it is already at the end of the message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send">
    You have five seconds to change your mind. See [Undo a send](#undo-a-send).
  </Step>
</Steps>

A few details about recipients:

- Each address becomes a chip that you can remove with its **X**.
- Text that is not a valid email address is left out without a warning, so check your chips before sending.
- Addresses are saved in lower case.

If you click **Send** without a recipient or a subject, a red strip says "To and Subject are required."

To close the composer, click the **X** at its top right (**Close compose**), or press Esc. Clicking outside the composer does not close it. Any change you have not saved yet is saved first, so what you wrote stays in **Drafts** (see [Drafts](#drafts)). If you changed nothing, the composer closes without saving a draft, unless the message opened with text already in it (see [Drafts](#drafts)). If the **Link** box is open, Esc closes only that box.

## Where the composer opens

The composer opens as a large window in the middle of the screen. The mail behind it is dimmed and slightly blurred. You cannot click the mail behind it until you close the composer. It looks and works the same on every mail page.

- The top bar shows the title, the draft status (see [Drafts](#drafts)) and the **X**.
- The message area scrolls inside the window. As your message gets longer, the line you are typing stays in view.
- For a new message, the cursor starts in **To**. For a reply, a forward or a draft you reopen, it starts at the beginning of the message text.

On a phone or in a narrow window, the composer is a panel that slides up from the bottom and covers most of the screen. The strip above it is dimmed. If the folder sidebar was open, it closes.

Only one composer is open at a time. If you start another message while one is open, for example with **Undo** on the send notice or an action in search, the new one takes its place. Any change you have not saved yet in the message you were writing is saved to **Drafts** first.

The composer's title tells you what you are writing:

| Title                                                             | Where it comes from                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **New message**                                                   | **Compose** in the sidebar, **m** then **c**, **Compose mail** in search, **Email** in a person's menu, on a contact card or as an action on a person in search, and **Attach to a new mail** on a Drive file in search.                                                                |
| "Reply to" and the sender's name, for example "Reply to Ana Lima" | The **Write a reply…** box in the **Reply** bar, **m** then **r** with a message open (not in **Drafts**), **Reply all**, a smart reply, **Reply with stance**, and **Draft reply** in the **Draft an apology** dialog. If the sender has no name, the title shows their email address. |
| **Reply**                                                         | **Reply** on a message in search, and a reopened draft that is a reply (see [Drafts](#drafts)).                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Forward**                                                       | **Forward** in the toolbar.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Draft**                                                         | **Edit draft** on a saved draft.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

When you click **Undo** after you send, the composer opens again with the same title as the message you sent. See [Undo a send](#undo-a-send).

A reply you start from search uses your default signature for replies and lands in the same conversation. It has no quoted message. Search opens the reply in the mailbox the original message is in, so **From** starts on that mailbox. On Outlook mailboxes, the reply lands in the conversation only when you send it from that mailbox (see [Replies from Outlook mailboxes](#replies-from-outlook-mailboxes)).

## Format your message

The bar under the message has these buttons:

- **Bold**, **Italic** and **Underline**
- **Bullet list** and **Numbered list**
- **Link**: add, change or remove a link (see [Add a link](#add-a-link))
- **Attach files**: pick one or more files from your computer
- **Link a file from Drive**: see [Link a file from Drive](#link-a-file-from-drive)
- **Signature**: pick another signature, or **No signature**. It shows only when there is a signature for the mailbox in **From**. See [Pick a signature while you write](/guide/mail/signatures#pick-a-signature-while-you-write).

If your workspace has a mail footer, it is added to the end of the message when it is sent. You do not see it in the composer. See [The workspace mail footer](/guide/mail/signatures#the-workspace-mail-footer).

### Add a link

1. Select the text you want to link. To change a link, click inside it. To add a new link with its address as the text, just place the cursor where it should go.
2. Click **Link**. A small box opens above the bar, with an address field and **Apply**.
3. Type the address, then click **Apply** or press Enter.

What **Apply** does depends on where you started:

- **With text selected:** that text becomes a link.
- **With the cursor in a link:** the box opens with the link's address filled in, and **Apply** changes it.
- **With nothing selected:** the address is added to your message as a link.

A few details about the address:

- If you leave out `https://`, it is added for you.
- An email address becomes a link that starts an email to that address.
- A phone number, for example `+1 555 123 4567`, becomes a phone link.
- If the address cannot be a link, the box says "Use a web, email or phone address."
- If you apply an empty field, the link is removed.

When the cursor is in a link, the box also shows **Remove link**. **Remove link** keeps the text and removes only the link.

Press Esc to close the box. Your message stays as it was.

Clicking a link inside the composer does not open it.

## Attachments

Click **Attach files** and choose your files. Each file shows as a chip with an **X** to remove it.

Attachments can be up to 25 MB per file and 25 MB in total. If one file is too big, a message names the file and says it exceeds 25 MB. If all files together are too big, you see "Total attachments exceed 25 MB".

## Link a file from Drive

Instead of attaching a file, you can insert a link to a file in one of your workspace's drives. The file is not attached: the person you write to opens it through your drive provider's link.

1. Click **Link a file from Drive** (the drive icon).
2. Pick the file.
3. Click **Insert link**. A link with the file's name is added to your message.

This button only appears when your workspace has a Google Drive or OneDrive drive. Other kinds of drives cannot share links this way.

If the file has no link you can share, you see "That file has no shareable link at its provider". If the file cannot be opened, you see "Could not read that file".

## Reply

Open the message. Click the **Reply** bar at the bottom of the reading pane to open it. It also opens when you scroll to the end of the message. Click **Write a reply…**. The composer opens with a title such as "Reply to Ana Lima", and with:

- **To** set to the sender, or to their reply address if they gave one. If you wrote the original message, the reply goes to its original recipients.
- the subject starting with "Re:"
- the original message quoted under your text, starting with "On (date), (sender) wrote:"

If there is a default signature for replies and forwards, it goes under your text and above the quote. See [Signatures and the mail footer](/guide/mail/signatures).

In **All inboxes**, **From** starts on the mailbox the message arrived in. The signature, the draft and the send follow that mailbox. The same goes for **Reply all**, smart replies, **Reply with stance** and **Draft reply**.

The quote is folded away. Click **Show quoted text** to see it and **Hide quoted text** to fold it again. Click the **X** next to it (**Remove quoted text**) to leave the quote out. Known trackers are always removed from the quote.

You can also press **m** then **r** while a message is open. It opens the same reply as **Write a reply…**. With no message open, it does nothing. It also does nothing on a message in **Drafts**. To keep working on a draft, use **Edit draft** (see [Drafts](#drafts)).

### Replies from Outlook mailboxes

A reply you send from an Outlook mailbox goes out as a reply in the same conversation, as long as **From** is the mailbox the original message is in. The person gets exactly what you wrote in the composer. Outlook does not add a quote of its own.

- If you change **From** to another mailbox before the draft is first saved, the reply goes out as a new message.
- If the original message was deleted, the reply goes out as a new message. You do not see a warning.
- In **All inboxes**, a reply starts **From** on the mailbox the message is in, so it stays in the conversation unless you change **From**.
- A reply you save as a draft is stored in Outlook as a reply. If you reopen it later with **Edit draft**, it is sent as a new message. See [Drafts](#drafts).

## Reply all

**Reply all** is in the toolbar when the message went to more than one person in **To** and **Cc**. It opens a reply like the one above, and also adds the original **To** and **Cc** recipients. Your own addresses are left out.

## Forward

Click **Forward** in the toolbar. The composer opens with:

- an empty **To** field
- the subject starting with "Fwd:"
- a "Forwarded message" block with the original's From, Date, Subject and To, followed by the original message
- the default signature for replies and forwards, if there is one, above the "Forwarded message" block

In **All inboxes**, **From** starts on the mailbox the message arrived in, and the signature follows that mailbox.

:::note
The original message's attachments are not included when you forward. To pass them on, download them and attach them to the forward yourself.
:::

## Reply from the bottom of a message

A **Reply** bar sits at the bottom of the reading pane. It does not show on messages in **Drafts**. It opens when you scroll to the end of a message, or when you click it. When it is closed, it may show how many smart replies are ready, for example **3 smart replies**.

Inside you find:

- **Reply with stance** and **Smart replies**, which write a reply for you with AI. See [AI help in mail](/guide/mail/ai-help).
- A box with **Reply to** followed by the sender's name, and **Write a reply…**. Click it to open the composer. The reply starts empty, with the original message quoted and folded away.

## Undo a send

When you click **Send**, the message waits for five seconds before it goes out. A notice counts down ("Sending in 5s…") with an **Undo** button.

- Click **Undo** to stop the send. The notice says the send was cancelled and your draft is restored. The composer opens again with your whole message and the same title it had, for example "Reply to Ana Lima" or **New message**.
- If you do nothing, the notice shows "Sending…" and then "Email sent". If a workflow added a BCC address to the message, the notice also says so, for example "A workflow added Bcc: archive@example.com", and stays on screen longer. See [Add a BCC to outgoing mail](/guide/workflows/add-a-bcc-to-outgoing-mail).
- If sending fails, the notice shows "Send failed" with the reason.

When you undo a send, the composer brings back the **From** mailbox, **To**, **Cc**, **Bcc**, the subject, your text with its signature, and all attachments. A reply keeps its link to the message it answers, so if you send it again, it still goes out as a reply in the conversation. If you were replying, the quoted message comes back as part of your text, not folded away. Your signature comes back as it was when you sent, so a signature you removed stays removed. No second signature is added.

If you send too many messages in a short time, you see "Too many requests. Please slow down." Wait a moment, then send again.

To send at a later time instead, see [Schedule a send](/guide/mail/schedule-a-send).

## Drafts

While you write, Metis Suite saves your draft with your mail provider about 1.5 seconds after you stop typing. If you keep typing, it still saves at least every 10 seconds. A save happens when you change **To**, **Cc**, **Bcc**, the subject, the text, the **From** mailbox (only before the first save) or the attachments, pick a signature, or remove the quoted text. Nothing is saved until the message has a **To** recipient, a subject or some text. Next to the composer's title you see "Saving draft…", then "Draft saved". If saving fails, it says "Couldn't save draft".

A reply you never change is not saved as a draft. The signature and the quote that are added for you do not count as changes. A message that opens with text already in it (a smart reply, a **Reply with stance** reply, **Draft reply** in the **Draft an apology** dialog, **Attach to a new mail** from search, or a message brought back by **Undo**) is saved shortly after it opens.

Your saved drafts are in the **Drafts** folder. To keep working on one:

1. Open **Drafts** and click the draft.
2. Click **Edit draft** in the toolbar. The composer opens. It shows "Loading draft…" while it loads the whole draft: the text, **Bcc** and the attachments. If the draft cannot load, it shows "Could not load this draft." It keeps saving to the same draft.

In **All inboxes**, **From** shows the mailbox the draft is in, and the composer keeps saving to that same draft.

You can change **From** only until the draft is first saved. After the first save, the **From** menu is turned off. If you point at it, you see "From can't change once the draft is saved". A draft you open with **Edit draft** is already saved, so its **From** menu is off from the start. To send from another mailbox, pick **From** before you start typing, or start a new message.

A reopened draft is titled **Draft**. If the draft is a reply, it is titled **Reply** instead. On Gmail, IMAP, Cloudflare Email, Resend and Amazon SES mailboxes, it keeps its link to the message it answers, so when you send it, it lands in the same conversation. The quoted original message comes back as part of your text, so you cannot fold it away again. To leave it out, delete it from the text.

What comes back depends on what your mail provider stores with the draft. On Outlook mailboxes, a reopened reply draft is titled **Draft** and is sent as a new message, not as a reply in the conversation. Also on Outlook, attachments you add or remove after the draft is first saved are not saved with it. On Zoho Mail mailboxes, a draft is saved without its attachments, and a reopened draft comes back without **Bcc**. On Zoho Mail, a reopened reply draft is also titled **Draft** and is sent as a new message, not as a reply in the conversation.

Reopening a draft does not add a signature. With Outlook mailboxes, a signature in a saved draft may come back as ordinary text. See [Drafts and signatures](/guide/mail/signatures#drafts).

When you click **Send**, the saved draft is removed. When you schedule a message, the composer closes. A draft that was saved while you wrote stays in **Drafts**.

On Zoho Mail and IMAP mailboxes, each save stores the draft as a new copy and removes the old one. In Metis Suite you keep editing the same draft, but another mail app may show the draft being replaced. The old copy is deleted for good. It does not go to **Trash**. When you send from a Zoho Mail or IMAP mailbox, the draft is also deleted for good.
