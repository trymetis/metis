---
title: Snooze and read later
description: Hide a message until a better time, bring it back early, and save messages to read later.
---

Two tools help you deal with mail at the right time. **Snooze** hides a message and brings it back when you choose. **Read later** keeps a list of messages you want to come back to, without moving them.

## Snooze a message

1. Open the message.
2. Click **Snooze** in the toolbar.
3. Pick a time: **Later today (+3 hrs)**, **Tomorrow 9 AM**, **This weekend (Sat 9 AM)**, **Next week (Mon 9 AM)** or **Pick date & time…** to choose your own.

The message is archived and leaves the list. At the time you picked, it comes back to the folder it was in when you snoozed it:

- From **Archive**, it stays in **Archive**.
- From **Trash**, it goes back to **Trash**.
- From any other folder, it comes back to your **Inbox**.

On Cloudflare Email and Resend mailboxes, a message snoozed from **Sent** or **Spam** comes back to that folder. In Gmail, a message snoozed from **Sent**, **Spam** or **Trash** stays visible there while it is snoozed.

:::note
Messages snoozed by the AI assistant or by a connected AI client (an MCP client) always come back to the Inbox.
:::

The **Snooze** button is not shown for messages in **Trash**. The snooze actions in the search dialog are offered for any mail result, Trash included. See [Results and actions](/guide/search/results-and-actions).

Some summary cards have a **Remind me** button, for example on a bill or a package. It snoozes the message too, and a notice tells you when it comes back. See [AI help in mail](/guide/mail/ai-help).

### Pick your own date and time

**Pick date & time…** opens a **Snooze until** window:

1. Type or choose a date and time in **When it comes back**, or use the calendar button.
2. Check the preview, which reads "Comes back" followed by the date.
3. Click **OK**, or press Enter. The message is snoozed until that time, like the other choices.

If the date is not valid, you see "Invalid date". If the time has already passed, you see "Pick a time in the future".

## See and manage snoozed messages

Open **Snoozed** in the sidebar. The heading shows how many messages are pending. Each row shows the sender, the subject and when the message returns, for example "Returns Tomorrow at 9:00 AM".

- **Wake now** brings the message back right away, to the same folder it would return to at the time you picked.
- **Cancel** cancels the snooze and leaves the message where it is, usually **Archive**. In Gmail, a message snoozed from **Trash** or **Spam** stays there.

If nothing is snoozed, the page says "Nothing snoozed." and reminds you how to use **Snooze**: the message comes back to the folder it was in, at the time you pick.

If something went wrong when a message was due back, the row shows "Wake-up failed" (or another status) in place of the return time, with the problem below it.

If a snooze fails, the message comes back to the list the next time it refreshes.

## Save a message to read later

1. Open the message.
2. Click **Read later** in the toolbar. The button changes to **Saved**.

To see your saved messages, click **Read later** in the sidebar. It lists every message you saved in this mailbox, from any folder. Saving a message does not move it. If you archive, snooze or delete it, it stays on the list. If a message is deleted for good, its row says "(message details unavailable)". Open it and click **Saved** to take it off the list.

Read later is personal. Other people who share the mailbox do not see your list.
