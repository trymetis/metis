A label tells you what kind of message you are looking at, such as **Work** or **Newsletter**. Each message has at most one label. The label shows as a chip on the message row and on the **Change label** button when the message is open.

Labels belong to the mailbox, not to you. Everyone who can see the mailbox sees the same labels.

## Built-in labels

Every mailbox has eight built-in labels:

| Label           | Row chip        | Tab                 |
| --------------- | --------------- | ------------------- |
| Action Required | Action Required | **Action Required** |
| Work            | Work            | **Work**            |
| Personal        | Personal        | **Personal**        |
| Transactions    | Transactions    | **Transactions**    |
| Updates         | Updates         | **Updates**         |
| Newsletter      | Newsletter      | **Newsletter**      |
| Promotional     | Promo           | **Promos**          |
| Social          | Social          | **Social**          |

A message that was sent from the **Scheduled** folder also gets a **Scheduled** chip.

## How messages get a label

A label can come from three places:

1. **AI labels.** When AI is on for the mailbox, Metis Suite reads new messages in your list and labels them for you. If it is not sure about a message, it leaves the message without a label and does not try again.
2. **Gmail categories.** For Gmail mailboxes, Gmail's own categories become labels: Promotions becomes **Promotional**, Updates becomes **Updates**, Social becomes **Social**, and Forums becomes **Newsletter**.
3. **You.** A label you pick replaces the AI label, and the AI never changes it back.

:::note
AI labels only appear when AI is turned on for the workspace and for that mailbox. Your workspace admin and the mailbox's owner control these settings. See [Workspaces](/guide/workspaces). With AI off, messages are not labelled automatically.
:::

## Change the label of one message

1. Open the message.
2. Click the **Change label** button in the toolbar. It shows the current label's name, or **Label** when there is none.
3. Pick a label. Built-in labels are under **Built-in**, your own labels under **Custom**.

## Label many messages at once

1. Tick the checkbox on each message you want to label. A bar appears with the number selected.
2. Click **Label** in the bar.
3. Pick a label from **Built-in** or **Custom**.

The confirmation reads, for example, "Labeled 3 emails as Work", with **Undo**. If you click **Undo**, you see "Reverting…" and then "Label change undone". If the undo fails, you see "Couldn't undo the label change".

## Create a custom label

Custom labels are your own names, such as a client or a project. They appear in the sidebar under the built-in labels.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click + next to Labels">
    Its tooltip says **Add custom label**. A small form opens in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type a name">
    Type it in the **Label name** field. Names can be up to 40 characters long.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a color">
    Choose one of ten colors: red, orange, yellow, green, teal, blue, purple, navy, gray or amber.
    Red is picked first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add label">
    Or press Enter. Press Esc, or click the **X** that replaced the **+** (**Cancel**), to cancel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Things to know about custom labels:

- Everyone who can see the mailbox sees your custom labels and can use them.
- If you create a label while in **All inboxes**, it is added to every mailbox.
- If a label with the same name already exists, no new label is added.
- Custom labels appear in the **Change label** and **Label** menus under **Custom**.

## Delete a custom label

1. Point at the label in the sidebar. On a touch screen, skip this step.
2. Click the **X** next to the label.
3. In the **Delete this label?** dialog, click **Delete**. The dialog tells you the label will be removed from your sidebar.

The label is removed for everyone who can see the mailbox.

Built-in labels cannot be deleted.
