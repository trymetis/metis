title: Read your mail
description: Find your way around mail, switch mailboxes, open conversations and save attachments to a drive.

Mail is where you read and answer email from every mailbox in your workspace. This page shows you how the screen is laid out, how to move between folders and mailboxes, and how to open messages and their attachments.

When you open mail in a workspace, Metis Suite starts in the Inbox of the first mailbox. If your workspace has no mailbox yet, you see a card that helps you attach one instead. See [Workspaces](/guide/workspaces) to add a mailbox.

## The mail screen

Mail has three columns:

1. **Folders** on the left.
2. **The message list** in the middle.
3. **The reading pane** on the right, which shows the message you open.

When no message is open, the list fills the space. You can drag the edge of each column to make it wider or narrower, and Metis Suite remembers the widths. The **Mail AI** panel can open as a fourth column on the right (see [AI help in mail](/guide/mail/ai-help)).

A message designed with formatting (an HTML message) uses the full width of the reading pane. This also applies to earlier messages you expand in a conversation. If a message is designed wider than the pane, it shrinks to fit, down to three quarters of its size. Past that, you scroll sideways to see the rest. A plain-text message keeps a line length that is easy to read.

On a phone, the screen works one column at a time:

- Tap **Open sidebar** to see your folders.
- Opening a message replaces the list. Tap **Back** to return to it.
- The composer is a panel that slides up from the bottom and covers most of the screen.

## Folders

The sidebar lists, from top to bottom:

1. The mailbox switcher.
2. The **Compose** button.
3. The **Mailboxes** section: **Inbox**, **Sent**, **Drafts**, **Starred**, **Trash**, **Read later**, **Snoozed**, **Scheduled**, **Subscriptions**, **Archive** and **Spam**.
4. The **Labels** section: the eight built-in labels, then any custom labels.

**Scheduled** shows a badge with the number of messages waiting to be sent.

## Switch mailbox

The button at the top of the sidebar shows the mailbox you are in. Click it to open the **Switch mailbox** menu. Each mailbox shows its color mark, provider and address, with a check next to the active one.

- Pick a mailbox to switch to it. You stay in the same folder.
- **Manage mailboxes** opens your workspace settings, where you add and remove mailboxes.

### All inboxes

When your workspace has more than one mailbox, the first item in the menu is **All inboxes** ("Every mailbox in this workspace"). It merges every mailbox into one view.

- Every folder, action and shortcut works in All inboxes.
- Each message row shows a chip with its mailbox's address, and a chip naming its provider when the message has no label, so you can tell them apart. See [Chips on a row](#chips-on-a-row).
- If one mailbox fails to load, the others still show. A banner names the mailbox that could not load, such as "Couldn't load ana@example.com. Try Refresh." Click **Refresh** to try again. When more than one mailbox fails, the banner lists every address.

## The message list

In the Inbox, **Read later** and the other folders that show a message list, the heading shows the folder name, with the **Refresh** button at the right end of the heading, before **Mail AI** when AI is on. Under it are the search box, a row of tabs and the list of messages.

Each row has two lines. The first line shows:

- a checkbox to select the message
- a star
- the sender's name, or their address when there is no name
- the subject
- a count when the conversation has more than one message

The second line shows:

- up to two chips
- a paperclip when the message has attachments
- the first words of the message, or the conversation's [summary card](/guide/mail/ai-help#the-compact-card), shown as the compact card
- the date, at the right

Unread messages are tinted and have a colored bar on the left. Messages in the same conversation are grouped into one row that shows the latest message.

### Chips on a row

The label chip comes first, when the message has a [label](/guide/mail/labels). In **All inboxes**, a row also shows a chip with the mailbox's address, then a chip naming the provider: **Gmail**, **Outlook**, **Zoho**, or **Mail** for any other kind of mailbox. In a single mailbox, rows show only the label chip.

A row shows at most two chips. When a message in **All inboxes** has a label, you see the label and the mailbox's address, then **+1** in place of the provider chip. You cannot open the **+1** to see the hidden chip.

### The paperclip

The paperclip shows when the latest message in the conversation has attachments. A conversation where only an earlier message has a file shows no paperclip. Pictures that sit inside the message text usually do not count as attachments.

### The summary on a row

When a conversation has a [summary card](/guide/mail/ai-help#summary-cards), its row in the list shows the compact card in place of the first words of the message. It is the same as the [compact card](/guide/mail/ai-help#the-compact-card) above the message: for example a package's arrival day, delivery steps and **Track**, or a code with **Copy code**. This needs AI to be turned on for the workspace and for the mailbox. In **All inboxes**, conversations from a mailbox with AI off show their first words.

The buttons on the compact card work from the list, such as **Copy code**, **Track**, **Join**, **Add to calendar**, **View order**, **Pay** or **Save contact**. Clicking a button or a link does not open the message. Clicking anywhere else on the row opens it. A few things show only in the reading pane: **Remind me**, **Unsubscribe** on a digest and the **Show summary** arrow. **Open email** shows only in an opened Link card. The compact card on a row never opens into the full card. To see the full card, open the message and click **Show summary**.

- A row never makes a summary itself. A summary is made when someone opens the conversation, or when Workspace AI or an AI app you connected summarizes it. Until then, the row shows the first words.
- When you open the conversation, the row switches to the compact card as soon as the summary shows in the reading pane. You do not need to reload the page.
- When [Workspace AI](/guide/assistant/use-workspace-ai) or an [AI app you connected](/guide/assistant/connect-an-ai-client) makes the summary, the row shows the compact card later. It shows when you reload the page, or the next time you open a conversation that shows a summary. It can show sooner: when new mail arrives in the list, when more rows load as you scroll, or when you come back to the list after more than 5 minutes.
- When a newer message shows in the row, the row goes back to the first words of that message. It shows the compact card again once the conversation is summarized again.
- Conversations with "No summary for this email" and conversations whose summary failed show the first words.
- If the person who runs your server changes the AI model used for summaries, rows show the first words again until the conversation is opened and summarized again. This can also happen after Metis Suite is updated.

### Check for new mail

The list loads more messages as you scroll down, and shows "end of list" when there are no more. You never need to reload the page.

The list checks for new mail on its own every 5 minutes while its browser tab is showing. It also checks when you come back to the tab, if the last check was more than two and a half minutes ago. So new mail usually shows within 5 minutes.

To check at once, click **Refresh** (the circular arrows at the right end of the heading). The icon spins while it works. Refresh updates the list you are looking at: the folder and its tab, the **Unread** or **Starred** tab, a label filter, your search results, or **Read later**.

The automatic check does not update the **Unread** or **Starred** tab, a label filter or a search. Click **Refresh** to update them. They also refresh when you switch to them from another list. Opening or closing a message does not refresh them.

If a refresh fails, a message at the bottom of the screen shows the reason, or "Couldn't refresh the list". The messages already in the list stay as they were. Click **Refresh** again to retry.

If you refresh or open mail very quickly, the reason can be "Too many requests. Try again in 20 seconds.", or the time left. Wait that long (at most 1 minute), then click **Refresh** again. See [Usage limits](/guide/reference/usage-limits#mail-calendar-drive-and-contacts).

If a list cannot load at all, you see "Couldn't load your inbox" (or the name of the folder you are in) with the reason and a **Try again** button. **Refresh** does the same as **Try again** there.

### Empty lists

If a folder has nothing in it, you see **No emails** and a note such as "Your inbox is empty" on the **All** tab, or "Nothing in this filter" on any other tab. With a label filter on, the note follows the tab that is underlined. If the folder has more mail to check, a **Load more** button shows under the note. Click it to check the next batch. On **Unread**, **Starred** or a label filter, grey loading placeholders may show while it checks. See [Find and filter mail](/guide/mail/find-and-filter-mail#tabs).

## Open a message

Click a row, or move to it with the keyboard and press Enter or Space. Opening a message marks it as read.

The reading pane shows:

- the subject, which stays at the top while you scroll
- the sender's name and address
- a recipients line, such as **To:** with the first two names. Click it to see the full **To** and **Cc** lists.
- the date, with small chips under it when remote images were blocked or trackers were removed (see [Privacy and safety when reading](/guide/mail/privacy-and-safety))
- the conversation's summary card when AI is on, as the compact card, or as the full card if you left a summary card open in this browser (see [AI help in mail](/guide/mail/ai-help#summary-cards))
- the earlier messages in the conversation, folded (see [Read a conversation](#read-a-conversation))
- the message itself, then its attachments, then smart replies and the stance buttons (**Accept**, **Polite decline** and the rest) when AI is on

Click the sender's picture, the sender's name or address, or a person in the full **To** and **Cc** lists to open a menu for that person. It offers **Open contact** if they are already saved, or **Add to contacts** if not, then **Email**, **Invite to an event**, **Search everything with** and their name, and **Copy email**. A small book icon shows who is saved already: a badge on the sender's picture, and an icon after each saved person in the **To** and **Cc** lists. See [Save contacts from mail](/guide/contacts/save-contacts-from-mail).

A long message is cut short with **Show full message**. Click it to read the rest, and **Show less** to fold it again.

The toolbar above the message starts with three icons: **Back**, **Previous message** and **Next message**. Point at an icon to see its name. Use **Previous message** and **Next message** to move through the list without going back to it. They are greyed out at either end of the list. When the list shows beside the message, it scrolls to show the row of each message you move to. **Back** closes the message. For the other buttons in the toolbar, see [Organize mail](/guide/mail/organize-mail).

When nothing is open, the pane says **Select a message to read**.

:::note
Messages in **Trash** show a banner about when they are deleted for good. Gmail, Outlook and Zoho Mail delete them after 30 days. For other mail servers, your mail server decides. Click **Restore** to move a message back to your Inbox.
:::

## Read a conversation

When a conversation has more than one message, the earlier messages appear as small cards above the open one. Each card shows the sender, the date and the first words. Click a card to expand it and read that message with its own attachments.

## Download an attachment

Attachments are listed below the message under a heading such as **2 attachments**. Each file shows as a card with a paperclip, its name and its size. Clicking the card itself does nothing. Two buttons sit on the right of each card:

- **Download** (the down arrow) saves the file to your computer under its original name.
- **Save to Drive** (the hard drive with a down arrow) copies the file into a drive. See the next section.

To download a file, click **Download** on its card. The arrow turns into a spinner while the file downloads. If the download fails, a message tells you why, or says "Couldn't download (file)".

## Save an attachment to a drive

You can copy an attachment straight into one of your drives without downloading it first. You need at least one drive that you can see in this workspace. If you have none, or Metis Suite cannot read your drives, the dialog says "Could not read your drives".

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Save to Drive on the attachment">
    It is the second button on the card. A dialog opens: "Choose the folder to save (file name)
    into."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a drive and a folder">
    Start from **Drives**. Each drive is listed by its
    [name](/guide/drive/browse-your-files#how-drives-are-named). Click a drive to open it, then
    click your way into the folder you want. Files show dimmed, and you cannot pick them. Click any
    name in the path above the list, such as **Drives**, to go back up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save here">
    **Save here** works as soon as you open a drive, so you can save at the top level of a drive or
    in any folder inside it. It stays off while you are at **Drives**. If the drive has a [folder
    restriction](/guide/drive/folder-restrictions) in this workspace, open one of its folders first.
    Saving at the top level of a restricted drive fails with "We couldn't find that." Click
    **Cancel** to close the dialog without saving.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The dialog closes at once, and the **Save to Drive** button on the card shows a spinner while the file copies. A message confirms it in the form "Saved (file) to (folder)". When you save at the top level of a drive, the folder part is the drive's name, such as "Google Drive · you@example.com".

If the save fails, you see the reason, or "Could not save that attachment".
