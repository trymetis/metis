---
title: Build your first workflow
description: Build, test and save a workflow that labels new mail from one sender shortly after it arrives.
---

In this tutorial you will build a workflow that puts the **Newsletter** label on new mail from one sender. You will start from a template, write a condition, pick a label, try the workflow on a real message, save it, and then see its runs in the history. It takes about five minutes.

You need a workspace with at least one mailbox. Pick a sender whose mail you are happy to see labelled, such as a newsletter. A label only marks the message. It does not move or delete it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Workflows page">
    Click **Workflows** in the left rail. The page lists your workflows on the left. If you have none yet, the list says "No workflows yet. Build one on the canvas, or start from a template."
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start from a template">
    Click **New workflow** at the top of the list. A menu of templates opens. Each template is a ready-made set of connected steps that you can change.

    Click **Label mail from a sender**.

    <img src="/images/workflows/new-workflow-menu.png" alt="The New workflow menu open with its list of templates" class="dark:hidden" />
    <img src="/images/workflows/new-workflow-menu-dark.png" alt="The New workflow menu open with its list of templates" class="hidden dark:block" />

    The canvas now shows three connected steps, from left to right:

    1. **Mail arrives**: the trigger. It starts the workflow for each new message in your first mailbox's inbox.
    2. **If**: the condition each new message has to meet.
    3. **Label**: the action, connected to the **Yes** output of **If**.

    Nothing is saved yet. The top bar says "Not saved yet", and the workflow only exists on your screen until you click **Create workflow**.

    The top bar also shows a red **1 problem** button, and the **Label** card says "Pick a label". You fix that in a later step.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Name the workflow">
    Click the name field in the top bar. It shows "Untitled workflow" until you type.

    Enter a name that says what the workflow does, such as `Label newsletters`. Names can be up to 80 characters.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the mailbox">
    Click the **Mail arrives** card on the canvas. Its settings open in the **Inspector** on the right.

    Under **Mailbox**, pick the mailbox to watch. The first mailbox in the workspace is already selected. Leave **Folder** set to **Inbox**.

    The card says "Watching" and the mailbox address.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Write the condition">
    The **If** step decides which messages go on to the label. Click the **If** card to open its settings in the Inspector.

    The **Condition** field holds a short expression: `item.from.contains("")`. Here `item` is the new message, and `item.from` is its sender's address. The expression is true when the sender contains the text between the quotes.

    Type part of the sender's address between the quotes, for example:

    ```
    item.from.contains("news@kestrelsupply.com")
    ```

    Below the field, the Inspector says "Gives yes / no", which means the condition is written correctly. It also says "This expression can't be shown as conditions; edit it as text." That is fine: this condition is meant to be edited as text. See [Expressions](/guide/workflows/expressions) for more you can write here.

    Messages that match go out of the **Yes** output to **Label**. Messages that do not match go out of **No**, which is not connected here, so nothing happens to them.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the label">
    Click the **Label** card. In the Inspector, open the **Label** list and pick **Newsletter**. Leave **Action** set to **Add**.

    The problem goes away, and the **1 problem** button disappears.

    <img src="/images/workflows/first-workflow-canvas.png" alt="The unsaved Label newsletters workflow: Mail arrives, an If step with a condition on the sender, and the selected Label step with Newsletter picked in the Inspector" class="dark:hidden" />
    <img src="/images/workflows/first-workflow-canvas-dark.png" alt="The unsaved Label newsletters workflow: Mail arrives, an If step with a condition on the sender, and the selected Label step with Newsletter picked in the Inspector" class="hidden dark:block" />

    :::tip
    Start with a narrow condition, such as one exact sender address. Once it is on, a workflow acts on every message that matches.
    :::

  </Step>

  <Step title="Try it without changing anything">
    Click **Test** in the top bar. The **Test run** tab opens under the canvas.

    **Test with** is set to "A recent item". Under **Sample**, pick one of the newest messages in the inbox, ideally one from your sender. Then click **Run test**.

    The canvas shows which steps the message reached. Nothing in your mailbox changes: if the message matches, the **Label** step only reports that it "would change this message".

    See [Test a workflow](/guide/workflows/test-a-workflow) for the details.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the workflow">
    Check that the switch next to the name says **Enabled**. Then click **Create workflow** in the top bar.

    If something is missing, a message appears instead, for example "Give your workflow a name" or "Fix the problem in this workflow before saving". Fix what it says and click **Create workflow** again.

    When the save works, the status next to the switch says "Saved", the button changes to **Save**, and your workflow appears in the list on the left.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Check that it is watching">
    Click the **Mail arrives** card. The Inspector shows how the mailbox is watched:

    - "Changes are pushed by the provider." means your mail provider tells Metis Suite about new mail.
    - "Checks for changes every few minutes." means Metis Suite looks for new mail on its own.

    After the first check, the line adds "Last checked" and how long ago that was.

    Mail that was already in the inbox when you saved is skipped. Only mail that arrives from now on goes through the workflow.

  </Step>

  <Step title="See it work">
    Wait for a new message from the sender, or send yourself a test message that matches your condition. Shortly after it arrives, the workflow runs and the message gets the **Newsletter** label.

    To see what happened, click **Activity** at the bottom of the workflow list. Each run of your workflow appears with its status and the steps it ran. Click a run to open it and see which messages each step handled.

    <img src="/images/workflows/activity.png" alt="The Workflow activity page listing recent runs" class="dark:hidden" />
    <img src="/images/workflows/activity-dark.png" alt="The Workflow activity page listing recent runs" class="hidden dark:block" />

  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you built

You built a workflow with one trigger, one condition and one action, tested it, and saw its runs in the history. Every workflow follows the same pattern, with more steps or different ones.

If this was only practice, turn the workflow off or delete it. See [Manage workflows](/guide/workflows/manage-workflows).
