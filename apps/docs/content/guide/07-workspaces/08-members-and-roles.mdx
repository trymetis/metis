Every workspace has a **Members** page that lists everyone in it. Each member also has a profile page, where you can get in touch with them and, if your role allows it, manage them.

## Open the Members page

You can reach the **Members** page in three ways:

- Click the workspace tile at the top of the left rail and choose **General**. Under **Members of** and the workspace's name, click **Manage members**.
- Open [search](/guide/search/search-your-workspace) and pick the command **Go to members**.
- On a member's profile, click **All members**.

The page says "Everyone in" followed by the workspace's name. Each row shows the member's name, email address and role. Click a row to open that member's profile.

## Roles

A workspace has three roles:

- **Owner**: there is exactly one. The owner can do everything, including changing roles, transferring ownership and deleting the workspace.
- **Admin**: manages members, invites and the workspace's settings, and can remove any resource from the workspace.
- **Member**: uses the workspace and adds their own resources.

For the full comparison, see [Roles and permissions](/guide/reference/roles-and-permissions).

Role changes, ownership transfers, removed members and invites are recorded. Owners and admins see them on the workspace's **Activity** page. See [Workspace activity](/guide/workspaces/workspace-activity).

## Invite people

Owners and admins can invite people from the **Members** page.

1. Click **Invite**. A dialog titled "Invite to" followed by the workspace's name opens.
2. Type an email address and choose **Member** or **Admin**. Add more rows with **Add another**, or paste several addresses at once.
3. Click **Send invite** (or **Send invites**).

Each person gets an email titled with your name and the workspace's name, with an **Accept invite** button. Invites expire after 7 days.

- A workspace invite works only for someone who already has an account on your Metis Suite instance, unless your instance lets anyone sign up. To invite someone who has no account yet, first send them an account invite. See [Account](/guide/account).
- Metis Suite does not show the invite link in the dialog, so the person needs the email to join.
- If you send too many invites in a short time, the dialog lists the addresses that failed.

:::warning[Treat invite links as private]
Treat an invite link like a password. Share it only with the person you invited, because anyone who opens it can use it.
:::

### Pending invites

Owners and admins see invites that have not been accepted yet under **Pending invites**, below the list of members. Each one shows the email address, the role and the date it expires.

To cancel an invite:

1. Click its trash icon (**Revoke invite to** and the address).
2. The dialog asks "Cancel the invite to" and the address. Click **Cancel invite**. To keep it, click **Keep invite**.

The link stops working right away. You can invite the person again at any time.

### Accepting an invite

When someone clicks **Accept invite**:

- If they are signed out, they see "Sign in to accept". They click **Go to sign in**, and after signing in they come back to the invite.
- If it works, they see "You're in!" and the workspace opens.
- If it does not, they see "Couldn't accept invite" with the reason: "Invite not found", "Invite already used" or "Invite expired". Ask an owner or admin for a new invite.

Every member can use these buttons:

| Button                                 | What it does                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Email**                              | Opens a new message to them.                                                                                                                           |
| **Invite to an event**                 | Opens a new one-hour event at the next full hour, with them as a guest.                                                                                |
| **Search everything with this person** | Opens [search](/guide/search/search-your-workspace) with a search for mail from or to them, and events they are invited to or organize.                |
| **Ask the assistant**                  | Opens **Workspace AI** with a new message that already holds this person as a chip. You see it only when AI is on for your instance and the workspace. |
| **Copy email**                         | Copies their email address.                                                                                                                            |

Below the buttons:

- **Recent mail with them** lists up to 5 recent messages from or to them.
- **Upcoming events with them** lists up to 5 upcoming events they are invited to or organize.

Click a message or an event to open it. You see only mail and events from the mailboxes and calendars in this workspace.

## Manage a member

Owners and admins see a **Manage** section on a member's profile. It shows only the actions your role allows. Nobody can change, transfer to or remove the owner from here, so the owner's own profile has no **Manage** section.

### Change a member's role

Only the owner can change roles. Use the **Member** or **Admin** picker in **Manage**.

- **Making someone an admin** asks for your authenticator code. The dialog "Make this member an admin?" explains what admins can do. Enter the code and click **Make admin**.
- **Changing an admin back to member** needs no code.

### Transfer ownership

Only the owner can transfer ownership.

1. Open the profile of the member who should become the owner.
2. In **Manage**, click **Transfer ownership**.
3. The dialog "Transfer ownership?" says the workspace will belong to them and you will become an admin.
4. Enter your authenticator code and click **Transfer ownership**.

### Remove a member

Owners and admins can remove members. Admins can also remove other admins. Nobody can remove the owner.

1. Open the member's profile.
2. In **Manage**, click **Remove from workspace**.
3. The dialog asks "Remove" followed by their address and the workspace's name. Click **Remove**.

The person loses access right away to every mailbox, calendar and drive in the workspace. The mailboxes, calendars and drives they connected themselves leave the workspace at the same moment, and stay on their own account. Their profile then says "This person is not a member of this workspace."

Owners and admins can also do these things from [search](/guide/search/results-and-actions#members): find the member, open the list of actions and pick **Make admin**, **Make member**, **Transfer ownership** or **Remove from workspace**.

## Leaving a workspace

There is no **Leave** button.

- **A member** asks an owner or admin to remove them.
- **If you are an admin**, open your own profile and click **Remove from workspace**. After that, you are taken back to your workspaces.
- **The owner** must transfer ownership first.
