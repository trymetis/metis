Each workspace has three settings pages for its resources: **Mailboxes**, **Calendars** and **Drives**. Open them from the workspace menu at the top of the left rail.

Each row shows the account's picture or provider logo and its email address. Under the address, a line shows the provider, the server's name when there is one, and the resource's short web address in this workspace. Calendar rows add how many calendars are shown, and drive rows add "whole drive" or how many folders are shown.

## Add a mailbox, calendar or drive

1. On the **Mailboxes**, **Calendars** or **Drives** page, click **Add mailbox**, **Add calendar** or **Add drive**.
2. Click an account you have already connected, or connect a new one.

For the full steps, see [Connect an account](/guide/workspaces/connect-an-account).

Anyone in the workspace can add their own mailboxes, calendars and drives. A newly added resource starts with AI and phishing detection turned off.

## Who sees a resource

Every member of the workspace sees every mailbox, calendar and drive added to it, whatever their role. You cannot hide a resource from some members of a workspace.

To keep a resource away from a workspace's members, do not add it to that workspace. If it is already there, [remove it](#remove-a-resource-from-the-workspace).

The choices of [which calendars a workspace shows](#choose-which-calendars-a-workspace-shows) and [which drive folders it shows](#choose-which-drive-folders-a-workspace-shows) apply to every member alike.

## Turn AI on or off for a resource

Each row has an AI control: a robot icon whose label reads, for example, "AI is on for this mailbox here" or "AI is off for this mailbox here". Click it to turn AI on or off. It controls whether the assistants and connected MCP clients can read this mailbox, calendar or drive in this workspace. Other workspaces are not affected.

- **Only the person who connected the resource can click it.** Everyone else sees a plain icon that shows whether AI is on or off.
- **Turning AI on asks for your authenticator code.** The dialog "Turn AI on for this mailbox here?" explains what AI will be able to read. Enter the code and click **Turn AI on**.
- **Turning AI off needs no code.** It happens at once.

The AI control appears only when your instance has AI set up. For more about AI, see [AI assistant](/guide/assistant).

## Turn phishing detection on or off for a mailbox

When your instance has phishing detection set up, each mailbox row also has a shield icon (its label reads "Phishing detection is on for this mailbox here" or "off"). Click it to turn detection on or off. When it is on, every message in the mailbox is sent to the phishing detection service to be scanned while the mailbox is in this workspace.

It works like the AI control: only the person who connected the mailbox can change it, turning it on asks for your authenticator code (click **Turn detection on**), and turning it off needs no code.

## When the workspace overrides a resource

A workspace's [AI and privacy settings](/guide/workspaces/ai-and-privacy) can be stricter than a resource's own setting. When they win, the row shows a badge:

- **Off here**: the resource's own setting is on, but the workspace turns this off.
- **Required here**: the resource's own setting is off, but the workspace requires phishing detection.

## Remove a resource from the workspace

1. Click **Remove** on the row.
2. Confirm in the dialog "Remove ... from this workspace?" by clicking **Remove**.

Removing takes the resource out of this workspace only.

- **A mailbox:** everyone in this workspace loses the mailbox and its workflows.
- **A calendar account:** this workspace loses the calendar and the choices of which calendars to show.
- **A drive:** this workspace loses the folder choices made for it. Your [pinned folders](/guide/drive/pinned-and-recent-folders) stay with the drive.

The account stays connected, keeps working in your other workspaces, and nothing is disconnected or deleted at the provider. To disconnect it completely, see [Manage your connected accounts](/guide/workspaces/manage-connected-accounts).

You can remove the resources you connected yourself. Owners and admins can remove any resource. As a member, you also see **Remove** on resources someone else connected, but only an owner or admin can remove those.

## Choose which calendars a workspace shows

A calendar account usually holds several calendars. You choose which of them this workspace shows.

:::note[A new calendar account shows nothing at first]
When you add a calendar account to a workspace, all its calendars start switched off. The workspace shows no events from it until you switch calendars on here.
:::

1. On the **Calendars** page, click the arrow at the end of the account's row (**Show calendars**).
2. Turn on the switch for each calendar to show, such as "Show Work in this workspace". **All calendars** turns them all on or off and shows how many are on, such as "2 of 3".
3. Click **Save**.

- The list marks your main calendar with **Primary**, and calendars you can only read with **Read-only**.
- A calendar added to the account later stays off until you switch it on here.
- Turning a calendar off hides it for every member of this workspace, and in this workspace only. It stays visible in your other workspaces, and the connected account keeps full access at the provider. This is not a security setting.
- The person who connected the account and the workspace's owners and admins can change these choices.

If the calendar sidebar says "None of your calendars are showing in this workspace yet.", click **Choose which calendars to show**. It opens this page with the right account highlighted.

## Choose which drive folders a workspace shows

A drive starts with the whole drive visible. You can limit a workspace to some of the drive's top-level folders.

1. On the **Drives** page, click the arrow at the end of the drive's row (**Choose folders**).
2. Tick the top-level folders this workspace can see. Leave everything unticked to show the whole drive. With nothing ticked, the panel shows "Whole drive is visible".
3. Click **Save**.

- This hides folders for every member of this workspace, inside Metis Suite only. It changes nothing at the provider.
- Only the person who connected the drive and the workspace's owners and admins see **Choose folders**.
- If the folders cannot be read, you see "Could not read the folders on this drive". A drive with no folders shows "This drive has no top-level folders yet."
