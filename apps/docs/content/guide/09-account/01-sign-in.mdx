title: Sign in

Every sign-in to Metis Suite has two parts. First you prove who you are with your password or a magic link. Then you confirm it's you with a code from your authenticator app, a passkey or a backup code, depending on what your account has. This page covers both, plus creating an account and signing out.

New here? Start with [Sign in for the first time](/guide/get-started/sign-in-for-the-first-time) instead.

## Sign in with your password

1. Go to your instance's web address. The sign-in page says "Sign in to manage your mail in one place".
2. Enter your **Email address** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign in**.

4. Confirm it's you, as described below.

Metis Suite then opens your default workspace. To pick which workspace that is, see [Choose where signing in takes you](#choose-where-signing-in-takes-you).

When you sign in, Metis Suite also emails you a sign-in alert, unless you turned alerts off. If someone else signs in to your account, you find out. To learn what the alert shows and how to turn it off, see [Sign-in alerts](/guide/account/sign-in-alerts).

## Confirm it's you

After your password or magic link, the page **Two-factor authentication** opens. It opens on your default way to confirm. That is the one you set up first (an authenticator app or a passkey), unless you chose another under **Ask me first with**. See [Choose which way you are asked first](/guide/account/two-factor-authentication#choose-which-way-you-are-asked-first). The line under the title tells you what to do.

Links under the code boxes, the backup code field or the **Use your passkey** button switch to the other ways your account has. You see **Use your authenticator instead** only if you set up an authenticator app, and **Use a passkey instead** only if you added a passkey and your browser supports passkeys. **Use a backup code instead** is always there.

### Use your authenticator app

If your account has an authenticator app, the page opens this way when it is your default, or when you click **Use your authenticator instead**. The page says "Enter the code from your authenticator app."

1. Open your authenticator app and find Metis Suite.
2. Type the six-digit code into the boxes.

     class="hidden dark:block"

3. To skip this step on this browser for a while, tick **Trust this device for 30 days**. Do this only on your own computer.
4. Click **Verify**.

If you see "Invalid code", wait for your app to show a new code and try again.

### Use a backup code

If you do not have your phone or your passkey, click **Use a backup code instead**. The page says "Enter one of your backup codes." and the field changes to a single box. Type one of the backup codes you saved when you set up two-factor authentication, then click **Verify**. Each backup code works only once.

If you see "Invalid backup code", check the code, or use another one. The code stays in the box, so you can correct a typo.

To switch back, click **Use your authenticator instead** or **Use a passkey instead**, whichever your account has.

Once you are in, replace what you lost and make fresh backup codes. See [If you lose your phone or passkey](/guide/account/two-factor-authentication#if-you-lose-your-phone-or-passkey).

### Use a passkey

If your account has a passkey, click **Use a passkey instead**. If a passkey is your default way to confirm, the page opens this way. The page says "Use the passkey you added to this account."

1. To skip this step on this browser for a while, tick **Trust this device for 30 days** first. Do this only on your own computer.
2. Click **Use your passkey**.
3. Confirm the passkey the way your browser or device asks.

There is no **Verify** button. Metis Suite signs you in as soon as your passkey is confirmed.

If you close the passkey prompt or wait too long, you see "The passkey request was cancelled or timed out." Click **Use your passkey** to try again. If you see "Passkey verification failed", try again, or use another way to confirm.

**Use a passkey instead** shows only if your account has a passkey and your browser supports passkeys. If you have passkeys but no authenticator app, and your browser cannot use passkeys, the page shows only the backup code field. A passkey does not replace your password or magic link. You always sign in with one of them first. To add a passkey, see [Add a passkey](/guide/account/two-factor-authentication#add-a-passkey).

### If something goes wrong

| Message                                                                                               | What to do                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Invalid code"                                                                                        | The authenticator code was wrong. Wait for a new code in your app and try again.                                |
| "Invalid backup code"                                                                                 | The backup code was wrong. Check it, or use another one.                                                        |
| "Passkey verification failed"                                                                         | Click **Use your passkey** again, or use another way to confirm.                                                |
| "Too many attempts. Sign in again to get a new challenge."                                            | Click **Cancel and sign out**, then sign in again with your password or a magic link.                           |
| "Too many failed verification attempts. Your account is temporarily locked. Try again in 15 minutes." | You entered 10 wrong codes. Your account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait for the time shown, then sign in again. |
| "Too many requests. Try again in 30 seconds."                                                         | You clicked **Verify** more than 10 times in a minute. Wait for the time shown, then try again.                 |

### Cancel

To stop without signing in, click **Cancel and sign out**. You go back to the sign-in page.

## Sign in with a magic link

A magic link lets you sign in from your email instead of typing your password.

1. On the sign-in page, type your **Email address**.
2. Click **Email me a magic link**.
3. The page shows **Check your email** and tells you where the link was sent. The link is valid for 15 minutes.

   <img
     src="/images/account/magic-link-sent.webp"
     alt="The Check your email message after asking for a magic link"
     class="dark:hidden"
   />
   <img
     src="/images/account/magic-link-sent-dark.webp"
     alt="The Check your email message after asking for a magic link"
     class="hidden dark:block"
   />

4. Open the email with the subject "Sign in to Metis Suite" and click the link.
5. Confirm it's you.

Each magic link works only once. A magic link signs in to an account that already exists. It never creates a new account. For your privacy, the page looks the same whether or not an account exists for the address you typed.

Typed the wrong address? Click **Use a different email**.

## Create an account

:::note[New instances are invite-only]
By default, a Metis Suite instance does not let people create their own accounts. The sign-in page then says "Accounts are by invitation only. Ask the instance owner or an admin to invite you." You create your account from the invite email instead. See [How invites work](/guide/account/invite-people).
:::

If your instance allows anyone to sign up, the sign-in page has two tabs: **Sign in** and **Create account**.

1. Click the **Create account** tab.
2. Enter your **Name**, **Email address** and a **Password** (at least 8 characters).
3. If your instance has terms, tick the box to agree to them.
4. Click **Create account**.
5. The page shows **Verify your email**. Open the email Metis Suite sent you and click the verification link. The link expires after 1 hour.
6. Sign in with your email and password.
7. Set up two-factor authentication. The steps are the same as in [Sign in for the first time](/guide/get-started/sign-in-for-the-first-time).

### If your email is not verified

If you try to sign in before you verify your email, you see "Verify your email before signing in." Click **Resend verification email** to get a new link. When you see "Verification email sent. Check your inbox.", open the email and click the link.

## If you try too many times

Each sign-in action has a limit. When you reach it, the page shows a red line under the form, such as "Too many requests. Try again in 1 hour." The time is how long you still have to wait. Wait for that time, then try again.

| Action                                               | Limit       | Longest wait |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ------------ |
| **Sign in**                                          | 10 a minute | 1 minute     |
| **Email me a magic link**                            | 5 an hour   | 1 hour       |
| **Create account**                                   | 5 an hour   | 1 hour       |
| **Resend verification email**                        | 5 an hour   | 1 hour       |
| **Verify** on the **Two-factor authentication** page | 10 a minute | 1 minute     |

For how the wait is shown, see [Usage limits](/guide/reference/usage-limits#what-the-message-says). For limits on resetting your password, see [Reset a forgotten password](/guide/account/change-or-reset-your-password#reset-a-forgotten-password).

## If your account is banned

The instance owner or an instance admin can ban an account. While your account is banned, you cannot sign in. Within 2 minutes of the ban, you are signed out everywhere. When you sign in with your password, you see "This account is banned. Ask the instance owner or an admin." You can still ask for a magic link, but opening it does not sign you in.

Some bans end by themselves after a set time. Otherwise, ask your instance owner or an instance admin to lift the ban. Nothing is deleted during the ban. Your workspaces, connected accounts and data stay as they were. Scheduled mail and snoozes you set up are held, and go ahead once the ban ends. Runs of your other workflows that come due during the ban are blocked, and stay blocked after it ends, until someone retries them. See [Cancel and retry runs](/guide/workflows/cancel-and-retry-runs#retry-a-run).

## Choose where signing in takes you

Signing in always opens your default workspace. At first, that is your personal workspace. If you created your account from a workspace invite, it is the workspace you were invited to. You can pick another one.

1. Open your account menu and click **Workspaces**. Your default workspace has a **Default** badge.
2. Next to the workspace you want, click **Make default**.

## Sign out

Open your account menu (the round button with your initials at the bottom of the left rail) and click **Sign out**. This ends your session in this browser only. It also clears the file previews Metis Suite saved in this browser.

To sign out of other browsers too, see [Manage signed-in devices](/guide/account/signed-in-devices).
