title: Desktop app

The Metis Suite desktop app opens your Metis Suite server in its own window, on macOS, Windows and Linux. Everything you can do in the browser works in the app, with a few extras such as a tray icon and a downloads folder.

To get the app, see [Download the apps](/guide/apps/download). It has the file for each system and the install steps.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your server address">
    The first time you open the app, it shows "Connect to your Metis Suite
    server". Type the address you use in the browser into **Server
    address**, for example `mail.example.com`, and click **Continue**.

    You do not need to type `https://`. The app adds it for you. If your
    server uses a port, keep it, for example `mail.example.com:8443`. The
    app ignores anything after the address, such as a page path.

    If the app shows "Enter your server's address, for example
    mail.example.com", the address is not valid. If it
    shows "No Metis server answered at" followed by your address, or
    "Could not reach that server.", check the address with the person who
    runs your server.

    An address that starts with `http://` works only for a server on your
    own computer (localhost).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in with your browser">
    Click **Sign in with browser**. Your usual browser opens. Sign in there
    as you normally do, with your password and your two-factor code.

    The app waits and says "Finish signing in in your browser. This window
    updates on its own." If you closed the browser tab, click **Open the
    browser again**.

  </Step>

<Step title="Confirm in the browser">
  The browser shows **Sign in to the Metis app**, with the account you are signed in as. Click
  **Continue as** your email address, or **Use a different account**. Continue only if you started
  signing in from the app yourself.
</Step>

  <Step title="Go back to the app">
    The browser shows **Back to the app** and opens Metis Suite. If the app
    does not open on its own, click **Open Metis**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The app has its own sign-in, separate from your browser. Signing out in the browser does not sign the app out, and signing out of the app does not sign the browser out.

Signing in to the app sends its own [sign-in alert](/guide/account/sign-in-alerts) email. It says **Desktop or mobile app, from a signed-in browser** next to **Signed in with**.

If the browser says **Set up two-factor first**, your account has no two-factor authentication yet. Follow **Set up two-factor authentication**, then start again from the app. If it says **Link not valid**, start signing in again from the app.

## What works in the app

- **All of Metis Suite:** mail, calendar, drive, the assistant and workflows, just as in the browser.
- **Connecting accounts:** connecting a Google, Microsoft or Zoho account opens your browser, then returns to the app.
- **Links** to other websites open in your browser.
- **Downloads** ask where to save, or go straight to a folder you choose in the app settings. Google Drive downloads open in your browser.
- **Print** and **View original** on a message open in their own app window. See [Print and view the original](/guide/mail/print-and-view-original).
- **When you go offline,** the window title says so. The app shows **You're offline** and reconnects when your network comes back. If your network works but the server does not answer, it shows **Can't reach the server**. Click **Try again** to check.
- **When loading is slow:** if Metis Suite has not opened after 15 seconds, the app adds "This is taking longer than usual." Click **Try again**, **Use a different server** or **Settings**.
- **The app remembers** its window size and position. With **Open where you left off** on, it reopens the last page you had open.

## Go back and forward

You can move back and forward between the Metis Suite pages you visited, as in a browser:

- In the **View** menu, choose **Back** or **Forward**.
- Press Alt+Left or Alt+Right. On macOS, press Cmd+[ or Cmd+].
- Press the back or forward button on your mouse.
- On a Mac trackpad, swipe left or right with three fingers. This works only when the trackpad setting **Swipe between pages** is set to swipe with three fingers, or with two or three fingers.

Back and forward skip the app's own screens, such as the loading, sign-in and offline screens.

## Update the app

The app tells you when a newer version of Metis Suite is out. It checks when it starts, and then at most once a day.

When a new version is out, your computer shows a notification. Its title names the new version, for example "Metis Suite 0.2.0 is available". Below it, the notification says "Open Settings to download it." Click the notification to open **About** in the app settings. You see each new version's notification only once. If a check fails, the app shows nothing.

You can also check yourself. Choose **Check for updates…** in the **File** menu on Windows and Linux, in the **Metis Suite** menu on macOS, or in the tray menu. The app opens **About** and checks right away.

Under **About**, the **Updates** row shows one of these:

- "You have version" followed by your version number
- "Checking for updates…"
- "Up to date."
- "Version" followed by the new version number and "is available."
- "Could not check for updates. Try again later."

Click **Check now** to check again. When a new version is out, **Check now** becomes **Download**. **Download** opens the right file for your computer in your browser:

- **macOS:** the `.dmg` file for your Mac's chip.
- **Windows:** the `.exe` installer.
- **Linux:** the `.deb` or `.rpm` file, to match how you installed the app.

If the app cannot tell which file you need, it opens the download page instead. The app never installs anything itself. To install the new version, follow [Update the apps](/guide/apps/download#update-the-apps).

To stop the checks, turn off **Check for updates** in **General**.

## App settings

Open the settings from your initials in the left rail, then **App settings**. You can also use **Settings…** in the **File** menu on Windows and Linux, in the **Metis Suite** menu on macOS, or in the tray menu. The setup, sign-in, offline and slow-loading screens also have a **Settings** link. **App settings** appears in the menu only inside the app.

The settings have five sections: **General**, **Server**, **Downloads**, **Advanced** and **About**.

### General

"How Metis Suite starts and closes on this computer."

| Setting                     | Default |
| --------------------------- | ------- |
| **Open when you log in**    | Off     |
| **Start in the background** | Off     |
| **Close to tray**           | Off     |
| **Open where you left off** | On      |
| **Check for updates**       | On      |

On Linux with GNOME, the tray icon needs the AppIndicator extension. Ubuntu turns it on by default.

**Check for updates** says "Asks downloads.trymetis.email once a day whether a newer version exists. Your server is never contacted." See [Update the app](#update-the-app).

### Server

Shows the server address. **Change server…** connects the app to a different Metis Suite server. **Sign out** signs this app out; your browser stays signed in.

### Downloads

- **When a download starts:** **Ask where to save** (the default) or **Save to a folder**. Click **Change…** to pick the folder.
- **Notify when a download finishes:** on by default. The notification says "Download complete".

If a download stops partway, the app shows a "Download failed" notification with the file name. It does this even when **Notify when a download finishes** is off. If you cancel a download, the app shows nothing.

### Advanced

- **Hardware acceleration:** on by default. Turn it off if the window flickers or stays blank, then click **Restart now**.
- **Clear cache:** clears the app's cache. You stay signed in.
- **Reset the app:** signs you out, forgets the server and restores every setting to its default. The app asks "Reset Metis Suite?" first.

### About

The app's version, and the Electron and Chromium versions it runs on. **Updates** shows whether a newer version is out, with **Check now** or **Download** (see [Update the app](#update-the-app)). **Log file** has a **Show** button that shows you the app's log file, which helps when you report a problem.
