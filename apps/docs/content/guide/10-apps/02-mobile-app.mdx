The Metis Suite mobile app opens your Metis Suite server on an Android phone. It shows the same Metis Suite you use in the browser, sized for your screen.

To get the app, see [Download the apps](/guide/apps/download). It has the file and the install steps.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your server address">
    The app shows "Connect to your Metis Suite server". Type the
    address you use in the browser into **Server address**, for example
    `mail.example.com`, and tap **Continue**.

    You do not need to type `https://`. The app adds it for you. If your
    server uses a port, keep it, for example `mail.example.com:8443`. The
    app ignores anything after the address, such as a page path.

    If the app shows "Enter your server's address, for example
    mail.example.com", the box is empty or the address is not valid. If it
    shows "No Metis server answered at" followed by your address, or
    "Could not reach that server.", check the address with the person who
    runs your server.

  </Step>

<Step title="Sign in with your browser">
  Tap **Sign in with browser**. Your phone's browser opens. Sign in as you normally do, with your
  password and your two-factor code. The app says "This screen updates on its own" while it waits.
</Step>

  <Step title="Confirm and go back">
    The browser shows **Sign in to the Metis app**. Tap **Continue as** your
    email address, or **Use a different account**. Then the browser shows
    **Back to the app**. If the app does not open on its own, tap **Open
    Metis**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Start signing in with the app already open, and keep it open until you are back.

The app has its own sign-in, separate from your phone's browser. Signing out in one does not sign you out of the other. If the browser says **Set up two-factor first**, set up two-factor authentication on your account, then start again from the app.

Signing in to the app sends its own [sign-in alert](/guide/account/sign-in-alerts) email. It says **Desktop or mobile app, from a signed-in browser** next to **Signed in with**.

## What works on a phone

- **All of Metis Suite:** mail, calendar, drive, the assistant and workflows.
- **Offline:** a thin banner shows when you are offline. If your network works but the server does not answer, the app shows **Can't reach the server** with **Try again**.
- **Slow loading:** if Metis Suite has not opened after 15 seconds, the app adds "This is taking longer than usual." Tap **Try again**, **Use a different server** or **Settings**.
- **Back button:** goes back through the pages you visited. When the app settings or a page opened in its own window are showing, the back button closes them instead.
- **Pages that open in a new window:** a link on your server that opens a new window shows the page full screen over the app. The top bar shows the page title and a close button. Close it to go back to where you were.
- **Connecting accounts:** connecting a Google, Microsoft or Zoho account opens your phone's browser. After you sign in at the provider, the browser shows the [confirmation page](/guide/workspaces/connect-an-account#confirm-the-connection). It names the Metis Suite account the app is signed in with. Its **Add to a workspace** list starts on **Don't add to a workspace**, so pick a workspace there if you want one. When you tap **Connect** or **Cancel**, you return to the app.
- **Downloads:** the file is saved to **Downloads**, with a notification. Google Drive files open in your browser.
- **Print** and **View original** on a message open full screen over the app, like other pages that open in a new window. **Print** shows the print page, but no print dialog opens. See [Print and view the original](/guide/mail/print-and-view-original).

## App settings

Open your account menu (your initials) and tap **App settings**. The setup, sign-in, offline and slow-loading screens also have a **Settings** link.

| Section      | Settings                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **General**  | **Open where you left off**. **Check for updates** (on by default). **Text size**, from 50% to 300% (default 100%), with buttons to make text smaller or larger.                                               |
| **Server**   | **Change server…** connects to a different server. **Sign out** signs this app out.                                                                                                                            |
| **Advanced** | **Clear cache**. **Reset the app** signs you out, forgets the server and restores the default settings, after you confirm "Reset Metis Suite?".                                                                |
| **About**    | The app's version, build, Expo SDK and platform. **Updates** shows whether a newer version is out, with **Check now** or **Download**. **Log file** has a **Share** button, to send the log with a bug report. |

The mobile app has no tray, start-up, hardware acceleration, zoom or download folder settings. Those are in the [desktop app](/guide/apps/desktop-app) only.

## Update the app

The app tells you when a newer version of Metis Suite is out. It checks when it starts. It checks again when you come back to the app, if a day has passed since the last check.

When a new version is out, the app shows an alert. Its title names the new version, for example "Metis Suite 0.2.0 is available". Below it, the alert says "Download the new version to install it over this one." Tap **Later** to close it, or **Download** to get the new APK in your browser. You see the alert for each new version only once. If a check fails, the app shows nothing.

You can also check yourself. Open **App settings** and go to **About**. The **Updates** row shows one of these:

- "You have version" followed by your version number
- "Checking for updates…"
- "Up to date."
- "Version" followed by the new version number and "is available."
- "Could not check for updates. Try again later."

Tap **Check now** to check again. When a new version is out, **Check now** becomes **Download**, which opens the new APK in your browser.

The app never installs anything itself. To install the new version, follow [Update the apps](/guide/apps/download#update-the-apps).

**Check for updates** in **General** says "Asks downloads.trymetis.email once a day whether a newer version exists. Your server is never contacted." Turn it off to stop the checks.
