---
title: Desktop and mobile apps
description: Use Metis Suite in its own window on your computer, or on your Android phone.
---

Besides the browser, you can use Metis Suite in a desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux, or in a mobile app for Android. You download the apps yourself, and they work with any Metis Suite server. Each app asks for your server address, you sign in through your browser, and the app keeps its own sign-in.

- [Download the apps](/guide/apps/download): pick the right file for your computer or phone, install it and check your download.
- [Desktop app](/guide/apps/desktop-app): sign in, menus, offline behavior and the app's own settings.
- [Mobile app](/guide/apps/mobile-app): sign in on your phone, what works there, and its settings.
