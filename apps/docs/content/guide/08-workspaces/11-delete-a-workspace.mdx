---
title: Delete a workspace
description: Permanently delete a workspace for every member.
---

Only the workspace's owner can delete it. The **Delete this workspace** section is at the bottom of the workspace's **General** settings page.

:::danger[This cannot be undone]
Deleting a workspace removes it for every member, at once. Its workflows, scheduled sends, assistant conversations and pending invites are erased, along with its settings for each mailbox, calendar, drive and address book.
:::

## What is kept

The mailboxes, calendars, drives and address books themselves are not deleted. They stay connected on the accounts of the people who connected them, and keep working in their other workspaces. Nothing changes at the providers.

The workspace's **Activity** page goes away with it, but the record of each change stays. You see the deletion, and the changes you made in the workspace, in your [Security activity](/guide/account/security-activity). Members still see the changes made to them in their own Security activity. The person who runs your Metis Suite server sees every recorded change in the server's audit log. These records are deleted when the time that person sets runs out.

## Delete the workspace

1. Click **Workspace settings** (the gear at the top of the left rail). The **General** page opens.
2. Scroll to **Delete this workspace**.
3. Type the workspace's exact name into the box that asks you to confirm it.
4. Click **Delete workspace**.
5. A dialog titled "Delete" followed by the workspace's name asks you to confirm. Enter your authenticator code and click **Delete workspace**.

Metis Suite then takes you to your default workspace.

## You cannot delete your only workspace

If this is your only workspace, the page says "You can't delete your only workspace." and the delete controls are hidden. Create another workspace first if you want to delete this one. See [Create a workspace](/guide/workspaces/create-a-workspace).

## If you connected an Outlook account

The delete section reminds you that anyone who connected Outlook should also remove Metis Suite from their personal or work or school Microsoft account. See [Outlook accounts: finish at Microsoft](/guide/workspaces/manage-connected-accounts#outlook-accounts-finish-at-microsoft).
