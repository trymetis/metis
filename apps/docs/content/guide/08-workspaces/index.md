---
title: Workspaces and accounts
description: Create workspaces, connect your mailboxes, calendars, drives and address books, and work with your team.
---

A workspace is where you and your team use mail, calendars, drives and contacts together. The accounts you connect stay yours, and you choose which workspaces use them. These pages explain how that works and walk you through each task.

- [About workspaces](/guide/workspaces/about-workspaces): what a workspace is, and how it uses the accounts you connect.
- [Create a workspace](/guide/workspaces/create-a-workspace): start a new workspace and invite people right away.
- [Switch between workspaces](/guide/workspaces/switch-workspaces): the left rail, keyboard switching, the order of your workspaces and your default workspace.
- [Connect an account](/guide/workspaces/connect-an-account): sign in with a provider or fill in a login form to connect a mailbox, calendar, drive or address book.
- [Add and remove resources](/guide/workspaces/add-resources): put mailboxes, calendars, drives and address books in a workspace, turn AI on or off for them, and remove them.
- [Manage your connected accounts](/guide/workspaces/manage-connected-accounts): see every account you have connected, and disconnect or delete them.
- [Fix an account that stops working](/guide/workspaces/fix-an-account): reconnect an account, and what an unavailable account means.
- [Members and roles](/guide/workspaces/members-and-roles): the Members page, inviting people, changing roles, transferring ownership and removing members.
- [Workspace activity](/guide/workspaces/workspace-activity): for owners and admins, see who changed roles, members, invites and resources in the workspace.
- [AI and privacy settings](/guide/workspaces/ai-and-privacy): decide whether AI and phishing detection are allowed in the workspace.
- [Logo and color](/guide/workspaces/appearance): give the workspace its own logo and accent color.
- [Delete a workspace](/guide/workspaces/delete-a-workspace): remove a workspace for every member.
- [Personal and shared settings](/guide/workspaces/personal-and-shared-settings): which settings are yours alone and which apply to the whole workspace.
