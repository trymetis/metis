title: Plans and prices
description: The Personal and Team plans, their monthly and yearly prices, and what each plan includes.
---

Metis Cloud has two plans, **Personal** and **Team**. You can pay for either one **Monthly** or **Yearly**.

## Prices

| Plan         | Monthly     | Yearly                       |
| ------------ | ----------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Personal** | $15 / month | $150 / year, two months free |
| **Team**     | $30 / month | $300 / year, two months free |

A year costs the same as ten months. The prices above are in US dollars. The [**Billing**](https://console.trymetis.email/instances/~/billing) tab of an instance shows the price before tax. Your invoices show the amount you were actually charged. See [Invoices](/cloud/billing/invoices).

## What each plan includes

- **Personal**: "For one person's mail, with included AI each month."
- **Team**: "About 3x Personal's memory and CPU, the same AI, and priority support."

Both plans include the same AI each month. You see how much of it you have used as a percentage on the instance's [**Overview**](https://console.trymetis.email/instances/~) (while it is running) and [**Billing**](https://console.trymetis.email/instances/~/billing) tabs. See [Included AI](/cloud/your-instance/included-ai).

## Choose a plan

You choose the plan and the billing period when you create an instance:

1. In the console, click [**Instances**](https://console.trymetis.email/instances), then **New instance**.
2. Under **Plan**, pick **Personal** or **Team**. **Personal** is selected at first.
3. Under **Billing**, pick **Monthly** or **Yearly**. **Monthly** is selected at first.
4. Check the **Summary** card. It shows the **Address**, **Plan**, **Billing** and the **Total**.
5. Click **Continue to payment**. You pay on the next page, through Dodo Payments.

For the full steps, including picking an address, see [Create an instance](/cloud/get-started/create-an-instance).

If the payment page cannot open, you see "Payment could not be started. Try again in a few minutes." Nothing is reserved. The address stays free, and you can try again.

## If you leave before paying

An instance you have not paid for shows the badge **Awaiting payment**. Its [**Overview**](https://console.trymetis.email/instances/~) shows **Waiting for payment** with "This address is reserved for you. Unpaid instances are released after 24 hours."

To pay, open the instance and click **Continue to payment**.

If the instance was paid for in the meantime, you see "This instance is already paid for."

## One subscription per instance

Each instance has its own subscription, and each payment pays for one instance. If you run two instances, you have two subscriptions.

## Change plan

The plan is fixed for the life of an instance. You cannot move an instance from **Personal** to **Team**, or back.

To use a different plan, create a new instance with that plan.

You can change the billing period of an instance while its subscription is active. See [Switch billing period](/cloud/billing/switch-billing-period).

## Where you see your plan

- The [**Instances**](https://console.trymetis.email/instances) list has a **Plan** column, for example "Personal, Monthly".
- The **Details** card on an instance's [**Overview**](https://console.trymetis.email/instances/~) has a **Plan** row.
- The **Subscription** card on an instance's [**Billing**](https://console.trymetis.email/instances/~/billing) tab has a **Plan** row.
