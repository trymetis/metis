Each Metis Cloud instance has its own subscription. You pay through Dodo Payments, and you manage everything else in the Metis Cloud console.

There are two places to look:

- The account [**Billing**](https://console.trymetis.email/billing) page lists every subscription and invoice on your account. Open the account menu (your name at the top right) and click [**Billing**](https://console.trymetis.email/billing).
- Each instance has a [**Billing**](https://console.trymetis.email/instances/~/billing) tab in its sidebar. This is where you change or cancel that instance's subscription.

These pages cover:

- [Plans and prices](/cloud/billing/plans-and-prices): the **Personal** and **Team** plans, monthly and yearly prices, and what each plan includes.
- [Switch billing period](/cloud/billing/switch-billing-period): move an instance between monthly and yearly billing.
- [Update your card](/cloud/billing/update-your-card): change the card an instance's subscription uses.
- [Cancel a subscription](/cloud/billing/cancel-a-subscription): stop an instance's subscription at the end of the period, change your mind, or subscribe again after it ends.
- [Invoices](/cloud/billing/invoices): see your payments and download each invoice as a PDF.
- [Missed payments](/cloud/billing/missed-payments): what happens when a renewal fails, and how to start a stopped instance again.
