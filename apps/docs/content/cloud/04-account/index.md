---
title: Account
description: Sign in to the Metis Cloud console, manage your password and two-factor authentication, and delete your account.
---

Your Metis Cloud account is the account you use to sign in to the console at `console.trymetis.email`. It is separate from the account you use inside your instance, such as `acme.metis-suite.app`. For that account, see the [User Guide](/guide/account).

You manage your console account under [**Settings**](https://console.trymetis.email/settings). Open the account menu (your name at the top right) and click [**Settings**](https://console.trymetis.email/settings). The page has the **Profile**, **Password** (or **Set a password**), **Two-factor authentication** and **Delete account** cards.

- [Sign in and passwords](/cloud/account/sign-in-and-passwords): sign in with email or with Google or GitHub, reset a forgotten password, set or change your password, and sign out.
- [Two-factor authentication](/cloud/account/two-factor-authentication): turn on a code from an authenticator app, save backup codes, and sign in with a code. You need it to download exports.
- [Profile and delete your account](/cloud/account/profile-and-delete-your-account): change your name, and delete your console account.
