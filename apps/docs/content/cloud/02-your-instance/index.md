These pages cover what you do with an instance in the console, and what is different inside a Cloud instance.

- [Overview](/cloud/your-instance/overview): the status of your instance, what to do next, and its details.
- [Instance mail](/cloud/your-instance/instance-mail): read the email your instance sends, and set up real delivery.
- [Included AI](/cloud/your-instance/included-ai): how much of this month's AI credit is used, and how to use your own provider.
- [Updates](/cloud/your-instance/updates): when Metis Cloud updates your instance, the emails it sends, and how to update or undo yourself.
- [How Cloud differs](/cloud/your-instance/how-cloud-differs): what works differently on a Cloud instance, and which Self-Hosting pages do not apply.
- [Export your data](/cloud/your-instance/export-your-data): download an encrypted copy of your instance and restore it on your own server.
- [Delete an instance](/cloud/your-instance/delete-an-instance): remove an instance and its data for good.
