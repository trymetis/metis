---
title: Export your data
description: Make an encrypted export of your Cloud instance, download it, and open or restore it on your own server with the metis CLI.
---

An export is one encrypted file that holds your whole instance. It is the same archive that `metis db export` makes on a self-hosted server. You can keep it as your own backup, or use it to move your instance to your own server.

The archive is encrypted to a public key. Only the matching private key opens it. You choose the key when you start the export: the console can make a key pair in your browser, or you can paste a public key you made yourself. Either way, the console never sees the private key.

Metis Cloud also backs up every running instance each night, but you cannot download those backups. An export is your own copy.

:::warning[An export holds your instance's keys]
An export copies all of your instance's data and its secret keys. Anyone with the archive and its private key can read all of it. Keep the private key somewhere safe, apart from the archive.
:::

## Before you start

You need two-factor authentication on your Metis Cloud account, to start an export and to download one. Without it, the [**Exports**](https://console.trymetis.email/instances/~/exports) tab shows **Two-factor authentication is required for exports**: "An export copies all of the instance's data to whichever key starts it. Turn on two-factor authentication in **Settings** to prepare and download one." **New export** and every **Download** button are disabled. Click [**Settings**](https://console.trymetis.email/settings) in that message to turn it on. See [Two-factor authentication](/cloud/account/two-factor-authentication).

You can start an export while the instance is **Running** or **Suspended**. Otherwise, the card says "Exports are available while the instance is running or stopped."

Only the Metis Cloud account that owns the instance can see its exports.

## Open the Exports tab

Open your instance in the console and click [**Exports**](https://console.trymetis.email/instances/~/exports) in the sidebar. The **Exports** card says: "An encrypted Metis archive of both databases and the instance's settings, the same one `metis db export` makes on a self-hosted server. Open it with its private key through `metis db load` or `metis export decrypt`. Download links work for ten minutes."

Click **New export** at the top right of the card. The dialog **New export** opens. It says: "The instance goes into maintenance for a few minutes while both databases and its settings are copied into one archive. The archive is encrypted to a public key, and only the matching private key opens it. This console never sees that private key."

The dialog offers two ways to choose the key:

- **Make a key in this browser**: "You download the private key as metis-export.key; only the public key is sent." This is the default.
- **Use my own public key**: "A P-256 public key from `metis keygen` or `openssl ec -pubout`."

Follow the steps for the option you choose.

## Export with a key made in your browser

Use this option if you do not have a key yet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make the key">
    In the **New export** dialog, keep **Make a key in this browser** selected and click **Make a key**.

    If you see "This browser could not make a P-256 key. Use your own public key instead.", follow [Export with your own public key](#export-with-your-own-public-key) instead.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the private key">
    The dialog shows the **Key fingerprint**, a code of 32 letters and digits such as `3f2a9c0d4b1e8a7f6c5d2e1b0a9f8e7d`. A fingerprint names a key, so you can tell keys apart.

    Click **Download metis-export.key**. Your browser saves the private key as `metis-export.key`. Store it somewhere safe, such as a password manager.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm that you saved it">
    Tick "I saved metis-export.key somewhere safe. Without it this export cannot be opened, and it cannot be downloaded again." You can tick it only after you have downloaded the key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the export">
    Click **Start export**. It stays disabled until the box is ticked. The dialog closes, and a new row with the status **Preparing** appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

:::warning[The private key cannot be recovered]
The console keeps no copy of the private key. If you lose `metis-export.key`, nobody can open this export, including you and Metis Cloud.
:::

If you close the dialog or click **Cancel** before you start the export, the key is thrown away. The next time you open the dialog, click **Make a key** again for a new one.

The `metis` CLI's `metis keygen` also names its private key `metis-export.key`. If you have both, keep them in different folders, or rename one, so you do not mix them up.

## Export with your own public key

Use this option if you already have a key pair, for example one you use for your self-hosted exports. To make one with the `metis` CLI, see [Make a key](/self-hosting/operate/export-and-import#make-a-key), or [Use OpenSSL instead](/self-hosting/operate/export-and-import#use-openssl-instead).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the option">
    In the **New export** dialog, select **Use my own public key**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the public key">
    Paste the public key into **Public key**, such as the contents of `metis-export.pub`. It starts with `-----BEGIN PUBLIC KEY-----` and ends with `-----END PUBLIC KEY-----`. Paste the public key only. The private key stays with you.

    The key is checked as you type. When it is valid, **Key fingerprint** and the key's fingerprint appear under the field. It matches the fingerprint `metis keygen` printed when it made the key.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the export">
    Click **Start export**. It stays disabled until the key is valid. The dialog closes, and a new row with the status **Preparing** appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A key made from a passphrase with `metis keygen --from-passphrase` works too. You then open the archive with the passphrase instead of a key file. See [Use a passphrase instead](/self-hosting/operate/export-and-import#use-a-passphrase-instead).

## While the export runs

Your instance goes into maintenance for a few minutes while the console copies it into the archive:

- People who open the instance see the **Down for maintenance** page, with the time maintenance began (for example "Since 10:00 UTC on 11 November 2026") and "Try again in a few minutes."
- Apps and scripts that call the instance get the status `503`. Webhooks that arrive get `503` too, so whether they arrive later depends on the sender trying again.
- Background work, such as workflows, waits until maintenance ends.
- The health checks answer as usual. See [Maintenance mode](/self-hosting/operate/health-checks#maintenance-mode).

When the export ends, whether it worked or failed, the instance comes out of maintenance on its own.

While an export is **Preparing**, the page refreshes on its own, and the **New export** button reads **Preparing** and is disabled. You can run one export at a time for each instance.

An export that is not finished 2 hours after you started it turns **Failed**.

Exports do not appear in the instance's audit log.

They do not appear on the **Exports** page of the instance's owner dashboard either. The console lists them only on its **Exports** tab.

## The list of exports

The **Exports** card lists your 5 most recent exports, newest first. Before your first export, it says "No exports yet."

| Column              | What it shows                                                                                               |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Time**            | When the archive was made, once it is ready. Otherwise, when you started the export.                        |
| **Status**          | **Preparing**, **Ready** or **Failed**. A reason can follow **Failed**.                                     |
| **Metis**           | The Metis Suite release the archive was made from, such as `1.4.0`. It shows "-" until the export is ready. |
| **Key fingerprint** | The fingerprint of the key the archive is encrypted to. It tells you which private key opens it.            |
| **File**            | When the export is ready, a **Download** button and the size of the file, such as "48.2 MB". Otherwise "-". |

If an export is **Failed**, start a new one. If the reason says to update the instance first, update it, then start a new export. See [Updates](/cloud/your-instance/updates).

## Download an export

When the status is **Ready**, click **Download** in its row. Your browser downloads the archive, a `.tar` file.

Each click makes a new download link that works for ten minutes. If a download does not start, click **Download** again.

## What an export holds

The archive holds:

- **The app database**: your accounts, workspaces, connections, settings and the rest of the instance's data.
- **The AI memory database**: the assistant's memory.
- **The instance's settings, with its secret keys.** Because of them, people sign in with their usual accounts and their connections keep working once you restore the archive.

The archive keeps your instance's settings for Metis Cloud's [instance mail](/cloud/your-instance/instance-mail) and [included AI](/cloud/your-instance/included-ai). These work only with Metis Cloud, so set up your own after you restore it. See [After you restore it](#after-you-restore-it). If you set up your own SMTP server, Resend or AI provider, those settings are in the archive too.

## Check and restore an export

You open an export with the `metis` CLI on your own computer or server. To install it, see [Install the CLI on your computer](/self-hosting/reference/cli-commands#install-the-cli-on-your-computer). The [Export and import](/self-hosting/operate/export-and-import) page in the Self-Hosting Guide covers each command in full.

In these examples, replace `export.tar` with the name of the file you downloaded.

- **Check the archive.** `metis export inspect export.tar` needs no key. It shows the release, the files and the recipients, and checks that the archive is complete. It lists 3 files. Its **Recipient** line matches the **Key fingerprint** column. See [Check an archive](/self-hosting/operate/export-and-import#check-an-archive).
- **Decrypt it.** `metis export decrypt export.tar --identity metis-export.key --out restored` writes the plain files into the folder `restored`. See [Decrypt an archive](/self-hosting/operate/export-and-import#decrypt-an-archive).
- **Restore onto a new server.** `sudo metis setup --from-export export.tar --identity metis-export.key --domain mail.example.com` installs Metis Suite from the archive. Pass your own domain with `--domain`. Otherwise setup uses your Cloud address. See [After you restore it](#after-you-restore-it). The server must run the same release as the **Metis** column, or a newer one. See [Restore onto a new server](/self-hosting/operate/export-and-import#restore-onto-a-new-server).
- **Load it into two new, empty databases**, for example on Turso, with `metis db load`. Pass your own address with `--app-url https://mail.example.com`. See [Load an export](/self-hosting/operate/export-and-import#load-an-export) and [When loading stops](/self-hosting/operate/export-and-import#when-loading-stops).

For an archive encrypted to a passphrase key, pass `--passphrase` instead of `--identity`.

You cannot import an export into a Cloud instance.

## After you restore it

A restored instance keeps its data, its accounts and its connections. Set these again:

- **Outgoing email.** If your instance sent its email through Metis Cloud's instance mail, the restored instance keeps sending it there until you set up your own mail service. That stops working once your Cloud instance is stopped or deleted. `metis setup --from-export` warns "The export sends mail through the Metis Cloud console, which works only on Cloud." Set up your own mail service. See [Outgoing email](/self-hosting/configure/outgoing-email).
- **AI models.** If your instance used Metis Cloud's included AI, set up your own AI provider. See [AI models](/self-hosting/configure/ai-models).
- **Phishing detection.** It stays off until you turn it on. See [Phishing detection](/self-hosting/configure/phishing-detection).
- **The address.** The archive holds your Cloud address, such as `acme.metis-suite.app`. Give the restored instance your own domain: pass `--domain mail.example.com` to `metis setup --from-export`, or `--app-url https://mail.example.com` to `metis db load`. Then follow [Move to a new address](/self-hosting/operate/export-and-import#move-to-a-new-address): register your sign-in providers' redirect URIs again, sign in again in every MCP client, and update your webhook addresses.

## If something goes wrong

### Starting an export

These messages show in a red alert in the **New export** dialog.

| Message                                                                                                                   | What to do                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "An export is available once the instance is running or suspended."                                                       | Wait until the instance is running, or until it is stopped.                                            |
| "An export is already being prepared."                                                                                    | Wait until the current export is **Ready** or **Failed**.                                              |
| "The export could not be started. Try again in a few minutes."                                                            | The export is listed as **Failed** once the page reloads. Wait a few minutes, then start a new export. |
| "Turn on two-factor authentication in Settings first. An export copies all of the instance's data to the key you choose." | Turn on two-factor authentication. See [Before you start](#before-you-start).                          |

### The public key

These messages show under **Public key** when you use your own key.

| Message                                                                                                                                    | What to do                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "This is a private key. Paste the public key (the .pub file); the private key stays with you."                                             | Paste the contents of the `.pub` file, such as `metis-export.pub`.                                                      |
| "Paste a PEM public key that starts with -----BEGIN PUBLIC KEY-----"                                                                       | Copy the whole public key, from its first line.                                                                         |
| "Paste one public key, ending with -----END PUBLIC KEY-----"                                                                               | Copy the whole public key, to its last line, and paste only one key.                                                    |
| "The key is too long."                                                                                                                     | A key can be at most 4096 characters. Check that you pasted only the public key.                                        |
| "The key is not valid base64."                                                                                                             | Part of the key was changed or cut. Copy it again from the file.                                                        |
| "The key's headers must be followed by an empty line."                                                                                     | Copy the key again from the file, with its empty line.                                                                  |
| "The key has a header this console does not accept: " followed by the start of that line                                                   | Remove that line, or copy the key again from the file.                                                                  |
| "The key repeats its Metis-KDF header.", or the same for Metis-KDF-Params or Metis-KDF-Salt                                                | Copy the passphrase key again from the file.                                                                            |
| "A passphrase key needs its Metis-KDF, Metis-KDF-Params and Metis-KDF-Salt headers."                                                       | Copy the whole passphrase key, with every header line.                                                                  |
| "Export keys are P-256 (prime256v1) public keys, as metis keygen or openssl ec -pubout write them."                                        | The key is another kind of key. Make a P-256 key. See [Make a key](/self-hosting/operate/export-and-import#make-a-key). |
| "The key is not a valid point on P-256. Export keys are P-256 (prime256v1) public keys, as metis keygen or openssl ec -pubout write them." | The key is damaged. Copy it again, or make a new one.                                                                   |
| "This key cannot be read."                                                                                                                 | Copy the key again, or make a new one.                                                                                  |

### Downloading

These messages show in a red alert in the card.

| Message                                                                                                                   | What to do                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "This export is not ready."                                                                                               | Wait until the status is **Ready**.                                           |
| "Export not found."                                                                                                       | Reload the page. If the export is still listed, click **Download** again.     |
| "This instance has been deleted."                                                                                         | The instance and its exports are gone, and the export cannot be downloaded.   |
| "Turn on two-factor authentication in Settings first. An export copies all of the instance's data to the key you choose." | Turn on two-factor authentication. See [Before you start](#before-you-start). |

## When the instance is stopped or deleted

When an instance is stopped, its status is **Suspended**, and its data is kept until the date on the [**Overview**](https://console.trymetis.email/instances/~). You can still start and download exports until then. For an export, the console starts the instance, makes the archive and stops it again. The instance stays shown as **Suspended** the whole time. See [Overview](/cloud/your-instance/overview#a-stopped-instance).

When you delete an instance, its exports are deleted with it. Download any export you want to keep first. See [Delete an instance](/cloud/your-instance/delete-an-instance).
