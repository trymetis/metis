description: Run your instance day to day with the owner dashboard, updates, health checks and backups.
---

Once your instance is running, the owner dashboard is where you watch it and change it. On a server the `metis` CLI runs, the CLI handles the jobs on the server itself. These pages cover both.

- [Owner dashboard](/self-hosting/operate/owner-dashboard): every page of the dashboard, from usage stats to settings history.
- [Upgrade and undo](/self-hosting/operate/upgrade-and-undo): move to a newer release, undo the last upgrade, update the `metis` CLI, and what happens when a step fails.
- [Health checks](/self-hosting/operate/health-checks): the endpoints for your uptime monitor or load balancer, and `metis status` and `metis logs` on the server.
- [Backups](/self-hosting/operate/backups): where your data and secrets live, what to keep safe, and how the upgrade snapshot differs from a backup.
- [Troubleshooting](/self-hosting/operate/troubleshooting): deploy checks, warnings and common problems.
- [Manage components](/self-hosting/operate/manage-components): add, change and remove the parts of a server the `metis` CLI runs, such as the updater, workers, object storage and mail gateways.
