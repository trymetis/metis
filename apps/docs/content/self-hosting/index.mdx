Metis Suite is open source, and you can run your own instance of it. This guide is for the person who runs that instance: the operator. It covers the server, the settings, and the owner dashboard. If you only want to use Metis Suite, see the [User Guide](/guide/get-started) instead.

An instance is one deployment of Metis Suite with its own database, its own accounts and its own settings. The people who use it connect their own mailboxes, calendars, drives and address books to it. As the operator, you decide which providers and servers they can connect to, which AI model it uses, and how it sends its own email.
